# Starfield Outpost Systems Research

How do Starfield outposts work, and what does a save actually preserve? This
research follows both questions across ownership, geography, production, storage,
power, freight, crew, construction and the outpost interface. The principal
investigated native and installed-definition build is **Starfield 1.16.244.0**.
Historical saves and observations carry their own provenance.

- **Understand** an outpost system through the [subsystem guides](docs/guides/README.md).
  Start with [why deposits can change](docs/guides/resource-generation.md),
  [how power is allocated](docs/guides/power.md) or [how ownership is established](docs/guides/identity.md).
- **Investigate** mechanisms and saved representations through the
  [technical references](docs/reference/README.md), [glossary](docs/reference/glossary.md)
  and [evidence access contracts](docs/reference/evidence-access.md).
- **Trace history** through the [history index](docs/history/README.md)
  and [research identifier lookup](docs/history/research-identifiers.md).
  Original reports and reviews preserve negative results and changed interpretations.

The [research roadmap](docs/research-roadmap.md) connects each subject to its
technical sources. The [first-release scope](docs/first-release-scope.md)
identifies the selected claims, available checks and deferred research.
