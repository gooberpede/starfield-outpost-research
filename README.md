# Starfield Outpost Systems Research

A version-bound research archive explaining Starfield's live outpost mechanisms
and saved representations together. The reviewed studies connect ordinary outpost
identity, geography, production, storage, power, freight, crew and construction.
They provide scoped explanations and preserve failed trials and unresolved cases.
This is a research reference; downstream software needs separate decisions.

**Publication: NOT CLEARED / NOT READY.** Development remains private pending the
[publication gate](docs/PUBLICATION.md). Research acceptance does not approve
release contents, evidence redistribution, visibility changes or publication.
Original authored work uses [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSING.md); see [notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and [credits](CREDITS.md) for separate material boundaries.

Start with the [current research roadmap](docs/research-roadmap.md). For each
subsystem it gives the best-supported explanation, meaningful limits, technical
references, evidence and independent review. The main reported native/definition
baseline is **Starfield 1.16.244.0**; historical saves and observations have their
own provenance and are not all revalidated on that build. The
[proposed claim/build scope](docs/audits/publication-readiness-b1/claim-build-matrix.md)
labels exclusions, unknowns and remaining approval gates.
