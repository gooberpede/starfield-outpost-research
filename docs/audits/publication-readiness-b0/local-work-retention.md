## Four-way classification

| Class | Retention rule | Concrete family and decision test |
|---|---|---|
| Protected originals and unique evidence | Preserve bytes, hashes and provenance; never overwrite to simplify cleanup. | Saves/screenshots, sealed capture/WAL, decoded evidence, original failed receipts and source snapshots tied to a scientific result. A duplicate pathname is not proof of equivalent evidence. |
| Source selected for potential promotion | Retain locally until exact version, authorship, dependency and content review; B3 chooses minimum needed sources. | Acquisition/orchestration helpers and scientifically significant historical revisions; compare declared historical hashes, not just current filenames. Promotion separately authorized; preserve original snapshot. |
| Uniquely significant local-only support/dependencies | Retain and label access/reproduction limits; review separate from public source. | Analysis databases/import state, lawful tool/game inputs, private integrity receipts and sole method/evidence locators. Rebuild cost and historical state matter even where bytes could be obtained again. |
| Demonstrably disposable scratch/generated duplicates | Propose exact manifest entries after reference/duplication and creator checks; no deletion by class alone. | Empty review-application trees or reproducible duplicate build output whose authoritative source and receipts remain protected. Age or suffix alone is insufficient. |

Classify mixed directories by individual file/subtree; never let a parent label
erase protected descendants. Preserve first, then separately decide selected
promotion, continued retention or deletion. Scientific value, privacy and rights
eligibility are independent classifications.

## Two-stage deletion procedure for a later brief

Stage 1 is an exact candidate manifest and review. Record resolved absolute path,
file/directory kind, measured files/bytes and measurement time; origin/creator and
source hash; references/consumers and scientific significance; authoritative
duplicate path/hash or reproducible regeneration evidence; protected descendants,
reparse-point/outside-root checks, preservation safeguards and proposed fate.
Record uncertainty rather than guessing duplication. Include pre-action retained
evidence/source hash inventory, available-space check, proposed recovery and stop
conditions. Empty directories require fresh recursive emptiness checks. A reviewer
assesses the named manifest, then the maintainer separately approves exact entries
and actions. A policy document or candidate manifest is not deletion permission.

Stage 2, only under that separate authority, re-resolves every target within its
approved boundary immediately before action and fails closed on changed contents,
new references, identity mismatch, unexpected links or protection/space failure.
Do not expand glob/prefix matches into additional targets. After approved actions,
verify preserved original/source/evidence hashes, receipt/reference availability,
actual removed/remaining paths, measured reclaimed file bytes and before/after
free space. Explain filesystem accounting differences and failures; empty trees
need not reclaim meaningful file bytes. Preserve the action log and review result.

No blanket `git clean -xfd`, cleanup by age, broad directory deletion, Git object
maintenance or implicit future approval. The ten RR1 temporary Git objects require
a separate repository-maintenance decision, not filesystem cleanup by B5 agents.
