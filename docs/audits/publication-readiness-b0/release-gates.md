## Required clearance versus improvements and exceptions

Required: scope/build agreement, misleading current guidance corrected, selected
claim method identity/access disclosed, exact rights/privacy clearance and
history/asset review, preservation and explicit publication authority. A historical
source absence can be disclosed with evidence tier and reduced claim scope;
it cannot silently satisfy a required reproducibility criterion. Maintainer must
approve material scope changes.

Recommended: broader roadmap polish, parameterization beyond selected current
entrypoints, space optimization and maintenance of unused scratch. They are not
invented blockers if required selected methods and safety are satisfied. B5's
creator gate matters where supported reproduction could recreate uncontrolled
outputs; unrelated cleanup can remain deferred with an explicit disposition.

Accepted historical exceptions, subject to B6 confirmation: protected local-only
payload references with accessible summaries/availability labels (RR1-011);
bounded auto-cleaned temporary tests (RR1-013); lawful installed dependencies and
identical installed Pascal copies (RR1-014); retained narrow GPL/SFSE boundaries
(RR1-017). Historical offsets, absolute evidence paths and superseded failures may
remain with clear wrappers and privacy acceptance. Exception is not wholesale
clearance of current instructions, game-derived evidence or personal paths.

## Version-update gate

The [baseline policy](versioned-baselines.md) applies before first release and
each later update qualification. Inventory changes first, reassess selected
definitions/interfaces/native dependencies and proportionate regression witnesses;
record each finding independently as REVALIDATED, CHANGED, CARRIED FORWARD — NOT
REVALIDATED or UNRESOLVED. Required questions need supported answers or explicit
scope revision. A Bethesda update alone neither orders research nor triggers a
tag. An independently reviewed qualification record plus maintainer approval
defines the release threshold. Keep older releases and historical locators intact.
