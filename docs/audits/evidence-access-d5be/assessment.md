of native member and completed row associations. It compared both ordered common
inputs, biome members, selector and recorded append, model identity, resource
pointer equivalence and FormIDs, constructor ordinals and final row census. The
read-only check and its result are retained locally alongside the source and
rebuild receipts. The comparison verifies the retained operand extraction for
the 346 recorded callbacks. The original phase boundaries and operator reports
carry their own provenance, and the sampled upstream generator and Flash render
remain outside the standalone row check.

derivatives or explicitly agreed claim narrowing, then B4-F1 membership and

release identifier, exact ref exposure, tags, hosted assets and timing belong
in the later run card. Publication stays **NOT READY / NOT CLEARED**.
