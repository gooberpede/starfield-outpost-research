## Substantive checklist, independent of typography

The implementer compared every table row against its cited report, including
the counterexamples above. Checks are document-to-document semantic comparisons,
**not executable engine tests**. Required reviewer questions:

- Does each condition, operand, order and exception have the cited accepted basis?
- Does the explanation preserve failed handles, cached eligibility, effective
  AV versus registration count, requested versus accepted output and unpowered states?
- Are shared backing, physical fixture identity, default stock and source consumer
  context kept distinct, including SD3's corrected default-zero preparation?
- Are recipe discovery, unlock retention, placement admission and STBH replacement
  separate without adding a total order, transaction guarantee or persistent-child promise?
- Are all unchanged candidates excluded for adequacy rather than an imposed quota?
- Are native source and scientific acceptance kept distinct from B2 wording review,
  rights/publication eligibility and live validation?

Actual independent dispositions and any counterexamples are retained in
[review](review.md). No decompiled function or proprietary payload is included.
