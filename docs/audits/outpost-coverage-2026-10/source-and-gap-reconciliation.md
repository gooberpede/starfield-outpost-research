## Scientific gaps versus publication defects

CR1 recruitment and capacity are a separate positive capability from assignment
serialization and skill lifecycle. [CR1's original answer table](../../experiments/crew/report.md)
and [capacity/admission](../../experiments/crew/capacity.md), 2026-10-18, establish
the ordinary available/assigned distinction, charged slots versus actor count,
destination capacity versus player command allowance and station quota versus
sampled assembly contribution. A60 records these as ALREADY RESOLVED within
mapped paths, including no separate combined global hire/assignment numeric cap
there. Zero-cost actors, script bypass and generic unassigned unload cleanup
remain explicit; arbitrary station disable/damage and command-reduction eviction
are unqualified. This repairs the audit's initial omission, not an old CR1 defect.

The central scientific claims inspected are generally expressly bounded: no
universal current-output, active-census or exact geography guarantee is needed
to use their scoped positive results. This audit found no justified reason to
reclassify all offline reference work as failed because actual runtime realization
is unmeasured. Such a promotion or downgrade would exceed the audit authority.

Two concrete release-impacting defects are proposed as PUBLICATION BLOCKER:
the stale global completed-research assertion (A52), and a promised reproducible
public package without an accessible allowed evidence path (A53). The latter is
conditional on including the affected claims in release scope: SD3's exact
review and native/movie receipts, position frozen/capture inputs and historical
donor evidence are deliberately machine-local. Existing documents disclose many
of these limits; they are not evidence of unauthorized current disclosure.
Qualification/minimal cleared evidence can resolve some defects without new science.

[PUBLICATION](../../PUBLICATION.md) separately requires agreed scope/build matrix,
rights/privacy and reachable-history review. The donor [permission entry](../../PERMISSIONS.md)
prohibits archive/save/substantial-payload redistribution and leaves publication
basis pending; this audit did not investigate new licence compliance or change
that basis. Raw assets, saves, captures and analysis databases remain LOCAL-ONLY;
authored research/audit remains REVIEW-REQUIRED. No item becomes CLEARED-FOR-PUBLICATION
through scientific confidence or reviewer ACCEPT alone.
