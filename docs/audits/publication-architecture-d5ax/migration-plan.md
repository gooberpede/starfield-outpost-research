# Ordered assignments and maintainer decisions

Each step is a separately bounded, independently reviewable package. Existing research and runtime permissions keep their scope.

| Order | Assignment and responsibility | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Maintainer reviews D5AX, B0 scope and D5AW decisions | Approve exact selected claim/build rows and advertised tiers, reader tree, approved archive memberships and history boundary for planning. Resolve census/dependency review findings before moves. |
| 1 | B1/B2 synthesis owner writes guide and reference pages at new paths | Independent reader and subject review. Every synthesis item maps to the original report, evidence, uncertainty and review. Superseded results are recoverable. No source relocation during this step. |
| 2 | B3 methods and B4/F1 evidence owners close selected dependency routes | Verify portable inputs, version and execution effects. Independently challenge indispensability. Confirm exact F1 membership, derivative sufficiency and item-specific privacy, rights, credits and asset eligibility. Hold or narrow any selected unresolved claim with explicit maintainer approval. |
| 3 | Migration owner applies approved archive and navigation plan | Verify guiding/source/metadata totals, exact bytes and safe member lookup. Replace current-facing inbound routes and review stubs. Package approved evidence only under its separately issued brief. B5 checks real scratch creators for the advertised procedures. Cleanup cosmetics remain optional. |
| 4 | B6 candidate owner stages an isolated validation environment under separate authority | Freeze candidate commit, intended refs, assets and manifests. Fresh checkout must have no access to existing local work, mapped private drives, local reports or pre-existing caches. Validate links/anchors with case-sensitive resolution, three reader journeys, method effects, lawful exact inputs, expected outputs and approved versioned asset/member hashes. Verify no supported procedure relies on private evidence. |
| 5 | B6 independent reviewer and maintainer review exact disclosure surface | Inspect actual tree, reachable proposed history/refs, contributor and upstream permissions/notices, privacy, derived payload, prospective tags and assets. Review current indicators and any candidate delta. Obtain exact release, history/refs, asset, visibility and timing authorization before any publication action. |
| 6 | Authorized release operator verifies actual hosted assets | After separate authorization to create the approved release and assets, test actual versioned URLs from an isolated client and compare downloaded container and extracted member hashes and bytes to the approved manifests. Re-review any resulting URL, asset or tree delta before final visibility or timing action where the approved publication sequence permits. |

| Maintainer choice | Proposed default | When it must be decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guide, reference and history tree | Approve the three entry paths and preserve experiments/reviews at current paths | Before synthesis contract |
| Archive groups and accounting | Approve the three bounded groups and the five-category accounting. Keep the current D5AT amendment and six other governing items loose. Decide whether the supplied D5AX brief should be integrated. | Before archive design freeze |
| Claim inclusion and advertised tiers | Select B1 ordinary runtime and persistence scope. Explicitly select which resource deltas belong. Preserve stronger historical holds unless sufficient approved evidence is supplied. | Before guides and final route qualification |
| Evidence and rights | Decide exact F1 asset membership, D5AS/AK/AL and contention derivatives where required, and acceptable authored table and excerpt scope. Review D5AT sampled raw atlas values and coordinates for appropriate granularity and underlying-content rights. Confirm actual author/contributor wording and SFSE notice compliance. | Before any proposed evidence packaging |
| Actual disclosure and history | Preserve ordinary main ancestry. Keep local Codex refs outside scope. Explicitly decide private path/filename and derived table exposure, and whether to include these audit datasets. | Before archive/migration freeze for affected items and again at exact B6 candidate |
| Release actions | Review exact tree, reachable history/refs, selected assets, visibility and timing as separate actions | After independent B6 assessment |

Settled constraints include disposable post-publication local working storage, exact unchanged first-party originals for any approved archive, exclusion of donor originals under current permission and the existing GPL-3.0-or-later policy. Previous independent reviews cover their own snapshots. The current delta is design evidence. It cannot substitute for the final candidate review. No later task requires authorizing new live research merely to compensate for a missing publication route.

The [D5AW register](../deferred-work-d5aw/dispositions.csv) assigns 12 pre-publication rows. P01 and P12 map to the scope and synthesis steps. P02 and P10 map to authoring, link migration and B6. P03 maps to methods and isolated qualification. P04, P05, P11, P13 and P14 map to item-specific evidence, author and rights decisions. P07 and P08 map to exact surface, asset and release authorization. This mapping preserves D5AW's deferred and on-demand scientific work separately from mandatory release preparation.
