export.** Its receipt and streams identify executions and expected refusals.

static continuation checker both changed after that export.

Use these evidence tiers throughout this audit.

| Tier | Evidence |
| --- | --- |

| B | Previously reviewed reproducible bounded result, such as D5BJ final02. |
