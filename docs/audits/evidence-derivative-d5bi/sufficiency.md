The minimum defensible tier retains every record in the four event journals,
with typed payloads sufficient to challenge all accepted N2 event relationships.
It also retains the occupied snapshot census and nested allocation/count evidence.
A winning-task timeline, an aggregate, or target-only lock extraction would lose
decisive contrary-history and cohort membership evidence.

scope of the recorded channels. Local reconciliation tests the projection
against that evidence. The independent reader recomputes the projected event
interpretation from retained operands. The uninstrumented result and native
counterfactuals outside the checked models are outside this tier. Historical B2
and the accepted N0/N1 limits remain separate evidence.

authorizes implementing the candidate within the brief. File-specific rights
