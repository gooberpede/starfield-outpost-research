**PUBLICATION DEPENDENCY — EVIDENCE ACCESS PENDING.** B4 must establish durable
routes for any original-witness claims retained at that tier, including I1/O2
Save52 joins, the controlled iron and local-link observations in I1/I2, F2's
sampled saved configurations, and P2's Save29 bytes and reported observations.
Decisive static assertions also need approved inspectable evidence or a tested
lawful regeneration route where a tracked explanation and locator alone are
insufficient. A lawful executable or ESM is a prerequisite for primary checks,
and a tracked CSV rerun does not reproduce those checks. Invocation-time Save29
events and actual organic knowledge or output were never captured by the cited
work. Packaging cannot recover those measurements.
