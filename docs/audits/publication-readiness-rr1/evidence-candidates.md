## Exact selected image checks

[screenshot-checks.csv](screenshot-checks.csv) identifies every selected CS/L3
image by source ledger row, exact filename, current local path, declared and
actual SHA-256. All **18/18** files exist and match. This proves byte identity
to those ledger entries, not who owns all depicted content, timestamp/game
state pairing or scientific interpretation. No pixel inspection or new image
rendering was needed to inventory their rights/reproduction role.

The first diagnostic guessed the wrong L3 root and recorded eleven unavailable
lookups. That failure is preserved locally in `screenshot-checks-initial.csv`.
Reading the actual witness ledger identified the protected screenshot root;
the corrected run checks the real files. No initial missing-path result is
used as missing-evidence proof. Original screenshots were never changed.

## Candidate decision record for B4

Each selected sample needs source/title and producer, exact file/hash, original
versus derivative relationship, embedded rights, privacy fields, minimum
scientific purpose, availability tier and explicit approval/exclusion. Record
the impact of unavailable originals separately from scientific confidence.
Maintainer/appropriate rights reviewer owns decisions; RR1 makes no legal
clearance determination or change to existing publication categories.
