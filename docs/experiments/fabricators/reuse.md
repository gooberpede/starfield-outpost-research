## Manufacturing boundary: modifiers

The [E1 502-PERK census](../inorganic-extractors/production-modifiers.md) and
[settled rank/actor composition](../inorganic-extractors/stacking-report.md)
are reused, not re-derived from descriptions. Manufacturing bases carry keyword
`0023ceb6`. The HasKeyword binding and OR-chain interpretation retain E1's
instruction/definition evidence. Factors multiply **duration**, not item quantity.
Zero-based PRKE indices and displayed/PRKR ranks are different domains.

| Source | Definition eligibility at the E3 timer boundary |
|---|---|
| Crew Outpost Management `0021b8c3` | Manufactured included in OR alternatives; ranks 1/2/3 give .95/.91/.87 |
| Crew Outpost Engineering `0021b8c8` | Manufactured-only predicate; ranks 1/2/3 give .91/.83/.77 |
| Engineering robot examples | Model A `001f416e` carries Engineering rank 1; participation still requires the shared eligible assigned-actor list. Robot identity is not an independent quantity multiplier |
| Management robot example | `001d9250` carries Management rank 1; same list/active-entry conditions |
| Garden `0004297e` / Sanitation `0004297f` | Inspected entries exclude ordinary manufactured bases (organic-only / inorganic conditions including manufactured false). No universal statement about every robot effect |
| Player Outpost Management `0023826f` rank 4 | .5 timer entry is gas/liquid/solid only; do not transfer it to manufacturing |
| Special Projects `0004ce2d` rank 4 | .95 A1 entry is inorganic-only. Recipe CTDA and construction implications are separate; no manufacturing quantity enhancement follows from that timer entry |
| Constellation Guide `00208e92` | Native ranks 4/5 .95 entries have no keyword condition; only actually active selected entries apply, not both merely because both definitions exist |
| Survey boost `0030b589` | Manufactured occurs in an inspected OR entry; another entry lacks a family gate. Additional predicates/acquisition still apply; no universal surveyed-planet bonus claim |
| Research Projects | Manufacturing construction recipes have research prerequisites. Reuse E1's inspected completion path: no direct timer grant was established; indirect equipment/crew access remains possible |

Handle-unique actor assignment, normal selected-rank replacement and per-actor
composition apply at the shared consumer. Distinct eligible actors can compose;
a list of possible crew/robots does not establish that all are active together.
Actual rank, assigned actor list, player perks, current predicates and effective
AV overrides are required for an instance's effective interval. Magazine and
survey factors are conditional participants, not exclusions based on a name.

For ordinary registration quantity 1 and positive duration D, `60 / D` is nominal
requested units per engine elapsed minute under sufficient inputs, valid reason
and successful destination assumptions. Input capping, scheduler batching and
insertion acceptance remain separate. No additive generic production bonus or
manual crafting speed has been inserted into this formula.
