The [earlier inline feasibility report](../spatial-equivalence-feasibility/report.md)
shows existing CALL hooks cannot see region terminal edges. The
[geometry-lifetime NO-GO](../spatial-equivalence-feasibility/geometry-lifetime/report.md)
adds a concrete missing owner fence, alias qualification and full-copy cost.
Borrowed worker pointers fail lifetime; extra references/locks mutate the target
and do not freeze contents; double reads do not exclude ABA. Synthetic owned
storage/stress tests qualify their own contract only, so no redundant synthetic
observer was built here.

The [debugger failure analysis](../event-latency/thread-control-final-report.md)
retains suspension/lifetime failures and queued owned traps delivered after
apparently clean restoration/detach. Original-byte checks and survival alone
did not qualify retirement. These are observed engineering failures, not merely
hypothetical hazards. C1 does not repair, rerun or revive either observer.

Minimum next qualification: native owner/alias ancestry through destruction;
positively observable population lifecycle/readiness identity; per-use operand
storage/preservation; checked owned-copy bounds/cold costs; loss and reentrancy;
exception/STOP quiescence. If those cannot be demonstrated under a separately
approved offline engineering contract, retain the blocker and offer manual
correspondence evidence under its own authorization. No claim of live readiness.

No external prior art was newly consulted or incorporated. Inherited owned
RG1/A1 methods retain provenance in their source ledgers; no new third-party
code, tool, dependency, contact or disclosure requires a permission decision.
