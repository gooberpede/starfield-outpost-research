Nineteen complete CALL edits plus two callback-pointer edits reuse already
qualified mechanisms. Extending the established CALL scopes avoids entry detours,
relocation of stolen instructions and fabricated calls. Optional radius/exclusion
predicate, internal marker creation and application-to-ship hooks were omitted:
they add intervention without being needed for the component oracle. Their
semantics remain unobserved. Request/command flags are raw decisions; visible
ship movement still requires operator evidence and cannot prove marker creation.

The specialist contributed four original travel files and independently traced
the two producer contracts; exact local note/hashes are retained in
.local-work/ship-relocation-050/specialist.md and specialist-hashes.json. The
independent reviewer inspected retained assembly rather than relying on that
summary. Original040 source is preserved;050 is an LF-normalized/adapted owned
project derivative at starting HEAD668504920120089a4c8cd86435e73554496c2f80.
Build.json pins every source, helper, frozen prediction and compiler/tool path.
Existing unmodified PluginAPI.h/MIT notice is covered by SOR-P-0001; owned code
by SOR-P-0002. Installed MSVC/MASM/SDK/CPython/system runtimes are separate tools;
no dependency or borrowed implementation was newly installed or incorporated.
Bethesda code informs this design; it is source-informed, not clean-room work.

[Schema5](../../../runtime/native-oracle-050/schema.md) defines every raw field.
Scopes own bytes, not deferred pointers. Identity sampling is non-atomic, with
race bit8 preventing qualification. Nested and concurrent invocations preserve
actual TLS ancestry; helper parent means enclosing route, not an invented full
native stack. Native outputs may disagree with replay; such disagreements are
results, not grounds to alter frozen predictions. The23 saved predictions remain
unchanged and full durable equivalence needs independent saved-to-loaded identity
and frame restoration. Numeric hierarchy replay does not validate opaque handles.

Startup admission edits only in the observed sole-thread pre-main phase after
all guards, module/file identities and disk checks. Unsafe admission waits with
an explicit receipt and STOP; no arbitrary clock. Once installed, patches and
RX leaf relays remain resident through capture STOP and normal exit. No running
game unhook/unload. Actual worker/write/guard/disk/queue-loss/depth failures close
admission. Heartbeat age alone does not. Shared memory observations and hooks
change timing/execution; disposable qualification cannot establish retail safety.
