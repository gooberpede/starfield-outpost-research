## What the native paths establish

The prospective caller derives indexed XYZ/context from a held preview reference
through RVA `0x00b4cee0`; it supplies operation-context bounds at +360/+364.
The established initialization caller resolves its Workshop and derives the
descriptor through `0x00f4a140`. That builder obtains radius via `0x00f38e30`
and constructs an upper scalar from center-height plus a setting or a selected
structure component's top. These are real source-resolution paths, not invented
signatures. Early descriptor branches and loaded lifecycle still matter.

The updater locks manager-owned ProcGen and visits its occupied source maps.
The resource callback resolves each resource identity and tests regions. Geometry
generation is a separate upstream provider/cache path, with component-selected
descriptors, source transforms, ordered resources, RNG and prior-region rejection.
The updater is not shown synchronously generating missing local terrain regions.
Therefore an initialized, adequately populated loaded world is a meaningful
prerequisite; a successful scan of an incomplete cache is not certified complete
natural availability.

The updater can retain spatial results across calls. Missing ATMO or BIOM
resolution retains those previous collections, rather than asserting an empty
population. A repeated resolved source identity also retains its collection.
The native AL return tracks refresh activity, not mathematical inequality of
sets. Radius and upper scalar are not compared in the mapped local spatial
refresh guard; caller resets may still invalidate them. No global invalidation
absence claim follows. [Dataflow](dataflow.md) specifies these branches.
