## Search basis and protections

Five protected mature originals were freshly hashed, strictly decoded and walked:
HIST-0035, HIST-0036, HIST-0210, HIST-0255 and HIST-0267. Selection covers mature
hab/storage constructions across more than Save52; HIST-0267 is the existing
mature Save52 identity, not an extra independent controlled action. Their source
hashes, full R3/REFR counts, selected-row counts, prefix gaps, body closures and
positive-edge totals are in samples.csv. All selected records have buffer 153;
the typed reader requires save format 27 and exact footer/type-run closure.
Original compressed inputs are unchanged; decoded scratch is context-cleaned.

The broader 278-entry frozen historical inventory is the selection catalogue,
not a claim that all 278 were decoded in BM1. Prior SD2/Workshop surveys retain
their missing/unsupported/older-format classes. BM1 directly rereads these five;
other originals, older formats, donor corpora and newly generated gameplay are
excluded from this scoped positive search. Their omission is not absence.

First-pass selection included behavior targets and named outpost/architecture
bases. A second pass added ordinary beacon 002c60e9 and dummy 015804, permitting
actual root/owner joins. Both receipts remain local as saved-* and saved2-*;
only saved2-* supports final counts. The initial archive assertion incorrectly
compared a bytes-keyed pin with a size-keyed historical metadata object. Exact
size and SHA256 agreed; the comparison was corrected before decoding. This was
a schema error, not a failed protected-input hash.

The selected reader preserves unknown extra suffixes, missing noninline bases
and default-derived state. It never byte-searches for a link tag or treats an
unsupported suffix as no variant. A zero B4 count in this selected population
does not refute the native primitive-base replacement branch: installed hab
walls use generated PKIN geometry.

## Optional discriminating observation

If later needed, the smallest wall test uses a protected **copy** of a known
ordinary hab save: identify one anchor/outer dummy/owner and its active generated
group, record all four sides, press CHANGE once, take a separate save and a
no-action control, then reload a copy and compare anchors, generated identities,
DA/191/DE and actual rendered sides. A second pair can connect/disconnect a hab.
Do not merge that experiment with damage, furnishings or power tests. It requires
separate allowed actions, source-save protection and any observer scope. Nothing
in BM1 executes it or demands new saves to accept the scoped offline result.
