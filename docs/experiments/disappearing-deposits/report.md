## Scientific discrimination

[D1-H1](hypotheses.md#d1-h1--saved-site-displacement) is falsified **for saved
site displacement across the three tested pairs**: pair/center/delta bytes remain
equal. This does not falsify invocation-specific native frame errors, and cannot
establish what happened outside these intervals.

[D1-H2](hypotheses.md#d1-h2--failed-construction-retained-as-visited) isolates
one concrete loss mechanism: cache insertion and visited bookkeeping need not
certify successful region construction. The exclusive construction lock rules
out the simplistic claim that these consumers race a partially written map under
that same lock. This mechanism alone cannot create a replacement Copper region.

[D1-H3](hypotheses.md#d1-h3--retained-operation-list) concerns stale area-resource
lists and their downstream selected-harvester gate. [D1-H4](hypotheses.md#d1-h4--changed-generation-inputs)
concerns changed source/descriptor inputs producing different current geometry
or type allocation. The saved data cannot choose between them. Both have explicit
obtainable falsifiers; neither surviving the offline tests establishes causality.

Installed definitions distinguish ordinary gas He-3 from atmospheric He-3.
The 22 distinct bases in the curated equipment set have none of the three special
property keys: HarvesterAtmosphere, SkipResourceVeinCheck or PlaceAnywhere.
Consequently these sampled ordinary gas/solid bases use the spatial-list branch
of the mapped gate under its normal resolved-property preconditions. An atmospheric
He-3 contribution in a whole-area list would not prove that a gas He-3 deposit
supports local placement. Missing historical He-3 equipment at VII-b prevents
assigning a particular extractor definition to the reported lost vein.

Saved base→resource associations and producer entries provide a concrete way
for installed configuration to persist independently of a contemporary placement
query. They support compatibility with the continued-production reports. **Actual
continued production, its cadence and whether every production path rechecks veins
were not tested.** A general producer survey is outside D1.

Public searches found repeated loss/substitution accounts and inconsistent reload
workarounds, but no inspected source documents the native cause or a verified fix.
SFCP #703 is open; its fetched current labels differ from the brief's Needs Vetting
description. See [the dated source ledger](public-sources.md). That search result
is bounded by inspected sources; it does not establish that no fix exists anywhere.

## Decision and stopping point

One integrated offline attempt is complete for review with **PARTIALLY DISCRIMINATED**
scientific disposition. Saved displacement is ruled out within measured pairs;
identity/resource controls and distinct native mechanisms are established. The
incident cause and exact affected-state snapshots remain unresolved. This does
not complete native spatial equivalence, E1/E2 research, or an integration contract.

The highest-value next observation would be a modest, separately authorized manual
comparison of Available Resources, ground scanner identity at fixed landmarks and
unplaced extractor eligibility at those same locations, before/after one return
to an already established site. It could falsify the **list-only** explanation even
without native instrumentation. It would not prove seed/cache causality. Missing
historical failure ground truth makes another broad save scan low value. A full
region-geometry observer remains costly and unqualified under the existing
[geometry-lifetime assessment](../spatial-equivalence-feasibility/geometry-lifetime/report.md).
No follow-on experiment, implementation, commit, push or publication is authorized.

reported symptoms with later observed geometry variation and preserves this
