## Table schema and domain qualifications

All geographic source keys are `chart:anchorX:anchorY:SFBK`, with decimal fine-cell
coordinates and an eight-digit main-file FormID. They are analytical keys, not
runtime pointers or epoch identifiers. A fine cell is 100 native units. The
descriptor is a geographic origin, while the source's generator origin is
`100 * (anchor - descriptor - 2)`. Query XYZ and disk radius use that translated
native frame. Native controls carry runtime-observed frames; saved cohort
requests retain the earlier conditional geographic derivation.

- `source-matrix.csv` has one row per modeled source in a successful-control halo,
  plus the 13 frozen mixed sources. Semicolon-separated captures express multiple
  control regions per source. Sources without a control remain included. Generated,
  positive-area and callback counts are distinct frozen quantities.
- `native-controls.csv` joins every one of the ten exact region controls to its
  matching frozen analytical source. Legacy receipt source-context absence is
  preserved; the geometric join does not invent a captured source descriptor.
- `feynman-lineage.csv` separates the whole linked-tree population, all installed
  component candidates and site-selected sources. Counts are table occurrences,
  not probabilities or live source counts. A fields hash covers concatenated
  ordered tag, little-endian uint32 length and decompressed field value, rather
  than the original compressed record. The enclosing ESM hash pins origin.
- `planet-context.csv` records all three successful planets' linked tree,
  resource-creation seed, system/body index and installed tree component names.
  Presence or absence of a named component in these records does not establish
  its historical runtime effect or an engine-wide mutation census.
- `query-domains.csv` measures nominal half-open geographic rectangles and the
  exact inherited conservative margin. The latter uses ranges
  `[anchor-4, anchor+nominal+5)`, preserving D5's inclusive endpoint convention.
  It is a tested neighborhood, not a proven caller-domain enclosure.
- `inverse_base_selector_cells` counts every fine cell in the anchor's coarse
  192-square tile for which the unchanged base selector returns the same key.
  Its masks test a conditional base-selection route. They do not establish the
  provider's virtual enumeration, effective manager frame or exact geographic
  round trip. They are neither a witnessed demand set nor a proof that the source
  cannot also be enumerated from another coarse cell.
- Reachable-mask lower bounds are empty where no specific first-miss query is
  established; upper bounds cover the body's installed low-nibble raster, where
  extracted, or the complete 0–15 codomain. These bound uncertainty under the
  installed-raster premise; they are not claims that every mask is reachable.
  Witnessed consumption and generation operands remain separate from original
  demand coordinates and sampled construction masks.
  `ordinary_weight_classes` groups the body's installed low-nibble values by
  their complete ordinary weight vector, in `source-matrix.csv` candidate order
  with Special omitted. Equal ordinary vectors imply deterministic ordinary
  output equivalence under otherwise fixed inputs; this does not qualify the
  high-nibble Special gate or prove those queries reachable.
- `alternative-equivalence.csv` compares the two D5A frozen ordinary masks per
  source. Ordered path equality compares resource, cell and points. Catalogue
  equality compares resources with at least one positive-area path; it is not
  equality of geometric union, callback predicates or final UI inventory.
