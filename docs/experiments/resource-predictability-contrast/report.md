# D5G: why exact replay and independent prediction differ

2026-10-25, Australia/Sydney. Offline-only [brief](../../implementation-briefs/README.md#archive-lookup) (`implementation-briefs.zip :: docs/implementation-briefs/CODEX_BRIEF_SOR_RG2_D5G_source_predictability_and_reachable_query_invariance.md`),
[methods](methods.md), [native proof boundaries](native.md), [review](review.md).

**The three successful sites have enough qualified installed inputs and witnessed
frames for exact conditional replay. Their measured source neighborhoods have
one ordinary mask each. The unresolved sites have competing neighborhood masks
that change ordered geometry, while the first admitted construction query remains
unknown. This is a real spatial distinction, but not a proved general rule about
all queries that can reach a source.** Native input selection also mattered:
Carinae required the installed surface tree and correct backlink resolution;
Archimedes required correct SFPT installation and component/map initialization.
The evidence supports multiple mechanisms, rather than a single biome-count cause.

| Question | Best-supported answer |
|---|---|
| Why were original Feynman V, Carinae and Archimedes exact? | Unchanged geometry arithmetic, source frames and independently derived installed selectors reproduce the finite witnessed paths under qualified component/context assumptions. The first construction query was not captured by those ten geometry controls. |
| What differs at second Feynman V? | Same PNDT/tree and Copper-family component as one side of the first site; different spatial SFPT slots/source instances and a nearby 0/3 mask boundary. Both frozen alternatives predict the same three resource names but different exact geometry. |
| Does the first site's biome boundary make its mask ambiguous? | Not in the measured nominal rectangles or conservative margins: all four are mask 8 across BIOM `0x000fc61c` and `0x00145440`. No proof covers every native first-miss query. |
| Which records matter? | PNDT FNAM→SFTR tables→SFPT installed arrays→typed SFBK, separate BIOM raster/mask, PNDT RSCS/PPBD, BIOM/RSGD/IRES initialization and root descriptors, system/body seed operands and generation settings/constants. Shared PNDT does not imply shared local pattern or mask. |
| Is there a general criterion for new outposts? | A sufficient conditional criterion is a uniquely derived effective input vector, or proof that all admissible vectors give identical relevant output. Neither complete native reachability nor historical context uniqueness is established here. One-biome location or a successful replay alone is insufficient. |
| What smallest fact would resolve the mask question? | A complete source-specific inverse of native query admission and sampling could prove invariance offline. Otherwise observe the selected source's first admitted miss, original Int2/effective descriptor and sampled mask, with a valid lifetime/consumer join. A current mask-3 geometry match does not provide that history. |

Confidence is PROVEN for the finite installed enumeration, hash checks, measured

The numerical paths and predicates are inherited frozen outputs, not newly
generated geometry. This assignment verifies hashes/framing and exact joins;
the ten controls were **integrity-checked, not rerun**. Generation code and
computational inputs are unchanged. Carinae's eight positive-area paths versus
seven callback accepts illustrate why intersection, callback and visible region
membership must remain separate. Only three of those accepted paths are native
controls; the rest do not become observed native extras.

The request body, descriptor/frame and consumer XYZ/radius include runtime
observations or saved conditional transformations. Component roots, slots,
candidate order, descriptors, installed tables and mask-neighborhood tests are
derived under the documented main-file initialization route. The initial seven
geometry receipts lack the later source-context field; their analytical source
joins must not become directly witnessed source descriptors. Archimedes's three
later receipts include source context. Separate C1G/D4A/D4D construction captures
constrain ordinary operands and map behavior, but do not retroactively join the
ten geometry controls to a first-query epoch. [Acquisition audit](../resource-construction-demand/acquisitions.md).

Thus the successful cases were not simply supplied an unknowable mask by their
geometry oracle. Their installed derivations have independent provenance and
measured local stability. They also do not establish unconditional historical
prediction: effective overrides, atmospheric/shared occupancy, alternate creation
routes and source lifetimes remain qualifications. Carinae and Archimedes's
earlier failures were demonstrated selection/preparation errors; they supply no
counterexample to the unchanged geometry arithmetic within the corrected scope.

## Controlled comparison of the two Feynman V sites

Fresh enumeration finds 1505 SFTR records in the pinned main file. The Feynman
PNDT `0x0005e0de` has one qualified non-BDST FNAM link, to SFTR `0x00168814`.
Enumerating both complete faces gives 131072 table cells and **16 distinct SFPTs**;
their serialized/installed arrays contain 114 distinct SFBK reference values.
The [lineage table](feynman-lineage.csv) retains every pattern/block occurrence
group and every installed PPBD→BIOM→RSGD candidate. This disproves a claim that
Feynman V has only one relevant installed pattern. It establishes one linked
tree on this main-file route, not absence of all regenerated or mutated trees.

The first site's coarse cells (130,168) and (130,169) select SFPTs `0x0023de88`
and `0x0023ddf6`. Its boundary is at fine Y=32448: BIOM changes from
`0x000fc61c` (Nickel-family root `0x000057cb`, RSGD `0x0037c672`) to
`0x00145440` (Copper root `0x000057cf`, RSGD `0x00258050`). Both raster sides
carry ordinary mask 8. Boundary crossing changes installed component selection,
but does not change the measured ordinary mask.

The second site's cells (195,108) and (195,109) select SFPTs `0x0023ddf4`
and `0x0023ddf3`, with flags `0x08`. All four source anchors have BIOM
`0x00145440`, the same root/RSGD/order as the first site's Copper side.
At fine Y=20928 the low nibble changes from 0 to 3 without a source-BIOM change.
The source `0:37536:20928:0029d20e` even shares its SFBK definition with one
successful original source; its anchor, frame, seed keys and mask context differ.
A different PNDT, different Copper component or different SFBK definition is
therefore unnecessary for this contrast. Different spatial SFPT routes really
exist, but no evidence makes them the sole cause of mask indeterminacy.

All three Feynman PPBD biome candidates and their effective/local RSGD references
are enumerated. The third BIOM `0x001a540d` / RSGD `0x000083de` is retained as an
installed candidate, not invented as a branch taken by either site. PNDT GNAM
system/body fields affect packed seeds; RSCS and PPBD affect component selection.
Neither an unrelated planet-record difference nor an unobserved branch switch
is needed to explain the measured contrast. Later component overlays and
historical effective content remain open, rather than disproved by main-file
enumeration. No installed plugin override census is claimed.

[Planet context](planet-context.csv) also records Carinae's installed
`SurfaceTreePatternSwapInfo_Component`; Feynman and Archimedes's linked SFTR
records contain no BFCB component entry. C2D did not establish that component's
retail consumer or historical effect. Its presence is an explicit open overlay
qualification, not a reason to replace the independently matching linked table.

## Nominal, admissible and witnessed domains

[Query domains](query-domains.csv) explicitly avoid assuming that nominal spatial
extent contains every admissible caller query. The 12 successful source margins
are single-mask: original V=8, Carinae=4, Archimedes=0. All 13 historical mixed
sources are also single-mask within their **nominal** rectangles and within the
tested inverse base-selector tile. Their conservative margins contain 0/8
(VI-b), 0/2 (VII-b), or 0/3 (VIII-b and second V). “Mixed source” remains the
frozen neighborhood classification, not a new claim of mixed nominal geometry.

The inverse base selector yields 144 cells per original/Archimedes/mixed source,
and 144 or 576 per Carinae source, all with a single mask per key. This is useful
evidence against equating the conservative margin with the selector's inverse.
It is not enough to remove any of the 13 frozen indeterminacies: native demand
looks up an index entry and dispatches its virtual enumeration list. That list,
effective manager descriptor and exact geographic sampling transform are not
proved identical to the inverse base selector. [Decisive static gap](native.md).

No inspected source is proved invariant over **all statically reachable first-miss
queries**. The lower bound on specifically established first-query masks is
empty; safe installed-raster upper bounds are V {0,1,3,8}, Carinae {0,1,4,8},
Archimedes {0,1,2,8}, VI-b {0,1,8}, VII-b {0,2,3,8}, VIII-b {0,1,2,3,8}.
These upper bounds do not assert reachability of each value. None of the ten
geometry controls, or D5B, supplies an original provider Int2/mask witness.
Witnessed later consumption is not an exhaustive demand domain. Generation
observations at other boundaries remain separately credited and qualified.

## Discrimination and generality

[Alternative comparison](alternative-equivalence.csv) uses the untouched D5A
26-run/13-source ordinary predictions. All 13 pairs have different ordered
paths. Twelve have the same resource-presence set in the modeled disk, including
empty sets for sources that miss it. Only VIII-b source `1:38112:960:0029d211`
changes presence. Source-wide geometry and consumer-level coincidence therefore
answer different questions.

At second V's selected source, masks 0/3 give 74/78 ordinary paths and 6/9
positive-area paths, respectively. IL/Fluorine/Copper counts change from
(2,15,57) to (25,35,18). Copper changes despite weight 1 in both cases because
earlier resources compete. [D5B](../resource-combined-acquisition/phase-b.md)
observed three exact current regions matching mask 3 and differing from mask 0;
it did not observe mask 3 being sampled or the historical generation epoch.

The [causal ledger](causal-ledger.md) rejects biome count, PNDT identity, resource
names and pointer retention alone as sufficient explanations. A useful future
prediction requires closing source/selection/frame/seed context and either
deriving the admitted construction input uniquely or proving output equivalence
over its admissible alternatives. The present data establish neither a necessary
single-mask condition nor an unconditional new-outpost guarantee.

The domain table also groups installed masks by ordinary weight vector. For the
original Nickel-family sources, masks 1 and 3 both give (0,1,1) in Water, Cobalt,
Nickel order. Mask 8 instead gives Water weight 0, Cobalt about 0.2 and Nickel
about 0.3. Every body-wide upper set in the table still admits at least three
distinct ordinary vectors; those upper bounds do not collapse to one prediction.
This finite input comparison demonstrates why mask uniqueness is stronger than
weight-vector uniqueness. It does not establish that masks 1 or 3 reach those
sources, and it makes no Special/high-nibble equivalence claim.

The smallest useful next test is **NOT AUTHORIZED**: for second V source
`0:37536:20928:0029d20e`, qualify the original provider Int2, effective descriptor,
source enumeration, first miss and sampled low nibble, then preserve the lifetime
join through consumption. A 0 versus 3 sample discriminates the frozen vectors;
a sample outside them falsifies the local-neighborhood enclosure. Cache-only
consumption leaves first-query history unresolved. Source replacement, manager
reset, missed decisions and incomplete construction are confounders. D5E's
qualification limits must be resolved under a separate assignment before any
live work; this report implements no instrumentation.

Scientific outcome is a finite positive contrast plus a scoped negative result
for reachable-query uniqueness. Analytical integrity, preservation, editorial
completion and independent disposition are recorded separately in [review](review.md).
No live target operated, so survival/restoration is not applicable. Seven
indeterminate historical associations, Save52 classifications and all D2–D5E
outputs remain unchanged. No new outside incorporation, disclosure or permission
