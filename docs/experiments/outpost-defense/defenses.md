The bounded negative is stronger than an unsuccessful search for a Defense AV.
The complete compiled selector enumerates its actual operands: permission,
planet identity/location, two timestamps, special CNDFs, 111 supply AV entries,
cargo-link AV and random/forced-roll inputs. Installed special-case configuration
has one Armillary entry. Story nodes/quest conditions select event/landing/biome
cases; the human native target collector consumes keyed membership and capability
flags. The complete unload loop consumes started state, living attacker count,
membership and target 3D. None reads a tower/turret count, robot/crew list or
defense statistic for probability or an abstract outcome comparison.

This supports **STRONG STATIC: no defense modifier within these mapped attack
selection and unload-resolution paths**. It does not deny physical defense,
other encounters, quest-specific overrides, DLC/mod definitions or every engine
consumer. Incidental hostiles may attack through ordinary combat without ever
passing SQ_Parent.CheckForAttack. No universal absence claim is made from names.

AttackOutpostObjects and GetDestructibleOutpostObjects positively converge on
the beacon's reverse WorkshopItem relationship, then bit 24/bit 23 filtering.
They do not scan arbitrary nearby scenery or target every Integrity UI bearer.
In particular, actor turrets have actor Health but the two saved turret snapshots
do not have the nonactor destructible capability bit 24 set in their saved form
flags. They are not thereby invulnerable; actor combat/damage is a separate route.
This distinction explains why Integrity, Workshop membership and membership of
this attack-specific destructible-object collection must remain separate facts.
