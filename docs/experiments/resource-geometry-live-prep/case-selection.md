silently selected to make four geometry-ready cases. Primary P3/P4 are held
preparation cards, not approved or runnable vein comparisons. R2 cannot yet
replace them scientifically. R1 has frozen geometry but does not replace mature
coverage. No more than four primary tests are proposed, and six sessions are
not required: P1/R1 share one save; P3/P4/R2 share another. Each reload/travel
still creates a distinct recorded epoch. Dropping mature coverage or promoting
reserves to additional live tests needs the RG2-B scope decision.

The single/mixed labels come from the accepted
[nominal biome study](../outpost-biomes/saves.md), not the outpost names.
Ownership means the sampled ordinary Workshop/construction association; active
player membership is UNKNOWN until confirmed. No completed mechanism is reopened
without a concrete counterexample.
