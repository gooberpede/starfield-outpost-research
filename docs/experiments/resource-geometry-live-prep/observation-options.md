## Prospective outcome interpretation

**PASS:** only the named observable correspondence, at actually qualified points
and error intervals, positive/negative/edge controls and recorded epochs. State
whether it concerns scanner appearance or extractor admission. It cannot become
native point equality, source completeness, persistence or integration safety.

**MISMATCH:** preserve the frozen prediction and raw observation; identify which
observable hypothesis disagrees. If the scanner's rendering product is unqualified,
report a correspondence mismatch, not automatic falsification of the native model.
A single fully qualified native operand/verdict counterexample could falsify the
corresponding scoped model; none has been acquired here.

**INDETERMINATE:** unknown identity/frame, unavailable sources, aim/position error
too large, confounding footprint/POI/Z restrictions, stale/unattributed refresh or
missing positive/negative control. Do not fit or relabel the frozen predictions.
Scientific result, completeness/integrity of evidence and target health remain
separate. A normal exit is not geometric validation.
