## Supported expansion

D2 inspected `family_access` to obtain ordinary families. That field describes
the normal cache-access route, while `common_assignment.family` also retains
guarded fallback assignments. Thus D2's four no-family projections were not
proof of missing native families. D3 uses the assigned family, searches the
effective RSGD and current BIOM descriptors, then follows the native alternate
component route where descriptor choice is invariant. It refuses distinct
alternate descriptors whose order is unresolved. These are definition-led
changes; no resource was selected because an extractor or screenshot named it.

Special Helium-3 is a geometric pass after ordinary resources. It uses its own
descriptor and the map's high nibble for the active/inactive multiplier.
The ordinary list retains descending rarity and the earlier competition state.
Native support and the separate Water routes are detailed in [operands](operands.md).
The D1 numerical generator, packed-key arithmetic and spatial predicates remain
byte-identical to the frozen bundle.

## Disposition of every unresolved class

Archimedes `D2-81a75b` still has four modeled sources selecting BIOM
`0x0028acd1`, RSGD `0x0037c66c`, Chlorine only. Its 306 generated paths are
unchanged. Tungsten is omitted before generation, while Chlorine is PRESENT.
The new [screenshot record](archimedes.md) independently corroborates visible
Tungsten and Chlorine availability with explicit observational limits.
No Tungsten-specific seed, BIOM substitution or spatial adjustment was made.
The next question is the actual source BIOM/component population, followed by
the selected descriptor; a new spatial predicate cannot repair an unselected
resource. Existing archival evidence does not distinguish changed effective
selection from retained historical geometry.

Feynman VI-b `D2-81bf2a`, active VII-b `D2-825f95`, VIII-b `D2-844cc6`
and the active second V site `D2-84e101` encounter mixed low-nibble masks.
Native code samples once from the construction query before the resource/X/Y
loops. It does not sample once per generator cell. D3 names the missing operand
`MISSING_FIRST_CONSTRUCTION_QUERY_MASK` and preserves positive results from
other supported sources. VII-b now has three corroborated positives despite
remaining partial; VIII-b retains Copper/Antimony and an unverified Fluorine
prediction. Gold remains indeterminate. No uniform-mask or per-cell shortcut
was substituted. [Acquisition](prospective-run-card.md) identifies the exact
query and mask operands needed for each affected source.

Feynman I's two fallback projections resolve to invariant alternate descriptors.
Feynman III's `0x000fd00a` projection instead has two distinct same-root Uranium
descriptors: BIOM `0x0008ae7b` → RSGD `0x000083ea`, and BIOM `0x00095092`
→ RSGD `0x000083dd`. D3 preserves
`UNRESOLVED_ALTERNATE_COMPONENT_DESCRIPTOR_ORDER`, rather than choosing the
first Python prediction row. The component-container lookup and its ordering
boundary were inspected; their selected live component sequence is not supplied
by the current projected corpus. A selected DNAM at the ordinary call directly
distinguishes these alternatives. Uranium/Vanadium remain indeterminate.

The three selected-Special projections now execute their supported geometric
pass. VI-b remains incomplete because of masks; VII-b supplies partial positives;
IX is fully supported. Special is not reclassified as atmospheric admission.
Nonzero Everywhere and truly absent assigned-family configurations remain
explicitly unsupported outside these tested projections. The native Special
pass is independent of an absent ordinary root, but the current corpus builder
conservatively stops a projection without a qualified assigned ordinary family;
no encountered selected-Special projection has that combination.

All 18 original indeterminate associations have individual rows in
[associations-final.csv](associations-final.csv): nine become corroborated,
seven retain the mask dependency and two retain descriptor-order uncertainty.
The two disabled cases remain excluded, including residual saved records.
No missing installed extractor is treated as missing terrain.
