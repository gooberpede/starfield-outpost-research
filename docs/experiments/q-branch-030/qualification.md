## Design and scope

[Rank 1 acquisition](../q-to-e/acquisition.md) supplied the bounded operand list.
The new implementation adds seven natural helper CALL wrappers to the existing
four CALL/two callback-slot observer, for **eleven CALLs plus two slots**. All are
complete instructions routed through RX FF25 leaf relays; compiler-generated
Windows unwind data covers wrappers. No stolen prologue, instruction detour,
forced native call, exception suppression, register/memory gameplay mutation,
external debugger revival or hot unhook/unload. The future observer necessarily
changes timing/stack use; it is not described as non-invasive.

[Schema 3](../../../runtime/native-oracle-030/schema.md) gives exact RVAs,
argument widths, sizes, domains and event kinds. Resolver/fallback return and
form/type, overlay+20/+24..30, actual A RCX landscape/+50/+54 and settings,
both 44-byte private buffers immediately after B, context[4]'s24-byte pointee,
and final 96F940 signed XY delta are copied synchronously under the owning Q.
Q world+310 is retained as eight **packed-value bytes**, correcting0.2.0's
unreadable-pointer interpretation prospectively; original captures stay unchanged.
The final candidate conversion AD0AF4 needs no added wrapper: final delta's actual
R9 input captures its resulting 44-byte hierarchy. Existing C/center/grid identity
channels and frozen predictions are retained.

An E-entry marker is emitted under the active Q before separate E admission.
STOP can reject a new diagnostic lease while the original E still naturally
executes, so marker and diagnostic scope are separate evidence. Already-admitted
Q helpers keep recording through normal completion/unwind after STOP; its lease
keeps retirement from declaring drain early. This avoids a false no-E conclusion.
No helper pointer survives the synchronous Q scope; queued records own bytes.
Reads have explicit addresses/length/status, bounded to 1024 bytes each. Shared
copies are not atomic; no ownership or safe-integration claim is introduced.

The decoder verifies normal Q pairing/return/latch, ordered stages/thread/ancestry,
resolver/candidate and owner/resolved/landscape/output/source argument linkage,
full private hierarchy tests in actual native order, journal/hash/read status and
worker completeness. Missing required operands, inconsistent linkage, truncated,
unhealthy or unwound records remain UNQUALIFIED. Equal A/B without E on a healthy
normal path supports the pinned CFG's bit 0 skip, with sampled flag disagreement
reported separately. A sampled key disagreement is also retained. Final delta
establishes reaching geometry; sampled rectangle bytes do **not** prove the exact
earlier XMM6 load or final rectangle arithmetic. No arbitrary absence of E implies
no enumeration elsewhere. Full ordinary-Workshop oracle still needs linked C.

## Preserved failures and remaining operational limits

qualification 01 passed 520 generic native checks, then inspector 30 had eight
ModuleNotFoundError errors because the versioned health helper was missing. Its
original log remains. Added the adapted helper and rebuilt fresh qualification 02.
An intermediate manual command used the wrong working directory, and an automated
fixture edit changed main's error return to a function-local name, producing
C3861; corrected before final qualification. Local preserved-failures.json clearly
distinguishes exact retained logs from manual-output transcription. No failed case
was overwritten or called a pass.

This task does not qualify live overhead, native branch-frequency/queue sizing,
game startup sole-thread feasibility with this new candidate, crash recovery,
or a guaranteed populated matching-site trigger. Existing 15 s worker health and
1 s nominal heartbeat remain; the earlier live strict 1 s monitoring FAIL is retained,
not requalified by synthetic tests. Queue capacity 4096 is a bounded buffer, not a
scientific event cap; saturation stops admission and reports loss. Per-record
FlushFileBuffers and terminal counters distinguish durable complete evidence from
failure. A future finite card must specify time/output bounds, save protection,
health cadence, retirement and exit policy before deployment.
