## The useful distinction

An **assigned** actor is in the destination population with the matching actor relationship. An **eligible** actor additionally resolves and passes life-state, Health, environment and consumer conditions, with the required active entry storage. A **contributing** actor actually has a passing registered entry in a particular evaluation. **Realized production** additionally requires a due row, current reason zero, ingredients where applicable and accepted product insertion. A Crew screen row, saved rank, assignment or arithmetic factor proves none of the later stages by itself.

The ordinary timer has no stored destination-wide Lin/Heller bonus that must wait for an arrival event. Its successive per-actor operations multiply the duration at evaluation. Stateful type1 destination abilities have a different refresh/removal path and can queue magic-effect work; their location/rank/competition gates do not replace the type2 timing mechanism. Ship-assigned actors provide a useful boundary: the outpost does not enumerate the ship's E2, and category2 timing also fails an inappropriate assignment environment.
