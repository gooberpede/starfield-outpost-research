## Scientific and operational outcomes

The [saved-position algorithm](../position-resumption/algorithm.md) already
recovers conditional saved centers with binary32 compensation and frame-specific
grid rules. It explicitly leaves future loaded-frame and same-invocation native
center/grid validation unknown. [D2](../resource-save52/report.md) also labels
radius 150 and Z+100 as static prediction parameters. D5K preserves these limits
instead of assigning those values to a new Workshop.

[D5B's native controls](../resource-combined-acquisition/phase-b.md) establish
three selected ACCEPT → same-evaluation transfer chains. They do not establish
all ordinary resources, all accepted regions, source lifetime, constructor mask
or an exhaustive negative. The new adapter parameterizes identity without
changing the underlying owned consumer and refuses incomplete ancestry. Native
chart-0 decoding is scoped; other charts do not inherit qualification.

The conditional wrapper imports the existing geometry, packed-key and spatial
arithmetic unchanged. It enumerates the supplied justified mask set without
using beacon location to choose a winner. Special helium-3, terrestrial/rarity-6
Water admission and atmosphere remain separate UNKNOWN branches. The historical
source-support premise comes from D5H/D5I, not from D5K's synthetic fixtures.
No ten-control, three-match, seven-association or 19-invariant/6-variable
historical classification is altered.

Scientific correspondence, engineering integrity, evidence preservation and
target survival are separate. Valid synthetic disagreements remain comparable;
positive historical matches do not repair readiness. Target survival is not
applicable: Starfield was neither launched nor attached. Only disposable offline
workers ran. Authored code/reports are REVIEW-REQUIRED; game/save/native/runtime
payloads and private receipts stay local. Publication remains NOT CLEARED.
