## Workstream A outcome

Only Exotic differs between the executable initializer and installed main ESM:
binary32 bits `0x3c23d70a` become `0x3d4ccccd`. The installed audit finds exactly
six relevant main-file GMST candidates and no matching candidate in the other
38 plugin files. None of the 23 inspected configuration files names these six
settings. This establishes the bounded installed-main resolution used here;
it neither proves an INI loader route nor excludes historical console changes.

The new versioned output preserves source keys, frame, biome, family/RSGD,
rarity order, masks, Cell chance, seed schedule, clipping and competition.
It changes the resolved settings inputs only. No seed search, source-specific
weight, translation or screenshot-derived parameter is introduced. The original
[evaluator contract](../resource-geometry/evaluator.md), frozen v1/v2 and C1E–G
receipts/results are retained unchanged.

Feynman has 154 identical resource/source/cell/geometry entries, two removals
and two additions. Both removed Fluorine paths have precisely the same ordered
points, widths and bounds as the newly admitted IL paths at cells (2, 6) and
(10, 1) of source `0:25116:32448:00257f38`. Resource identity therefore matters:
an unchanged total of 156 paths does not mean an unchanged population.
Whole-source order and competition produce this result; paths are not relabelled
by the implementation. Copper retains its 46 paths and exact native singleton.

At the retained C1E query, the model predicts one positive path for each of the
four accepted resources and no additional positive resource. All 961 visited
native rejections replay as false with their own captured geometry; four
acceptances replay as true. Five modeled paths have no exact visited native
geometry/resource witness. Acceptance short-circuits later regions, so these
checks cannot prove a complete native census or universal absence of extras.

Carinae retains 259 Iron, 505 Alkanes and 131 Rothicite paths. Its uniform mask
4 selects active weight 1 for all three ordinary rarities, including Unique
Rothicite. Applying an inactive Unique value of zero here would be an error.
Iron still has zero bounded positive-area intersections with the nominal
150-unit circle and misses the saved extractor by a nearest native-bound gap
of 233.29781343389072 model units. All three saved Carinae hardware points miss
the modeled bounded and tapered footprints. Presence agreement for Alkanes and
Rothicite is useful context, not exact geometry agreement.
