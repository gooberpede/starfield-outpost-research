Saved positions and exact joins come from the pinned [F1 witnesses](../resource-geometry-mature-context/f1-witnesses.csv),

The earlier screenshots remain qualitative evidence of Iron and multiple
patches; no image boundary is converted into precise geometry.

Installed inactive settings cannot repair this case while the frozen mask,
source and effective DNAM assumptions hold. Remaining unknowns are (1) actual
source/mask selection and effective RSGD/DNAM, then (2) geometry or competition
given equal operands, (3) consuming frame/height/footprint, and (4) cache
lifecycle/retention. No evidence here ranks a particular lifecycle bug as fact.
The smallest independent discriminator is whether actual Carinae source/mask/
selected DNAM equals the frozen request. Unequal operands would localize the
first divergence before geometry; equal operands would leave geometry/consumer/
lifecycle comparison. Acquiring those operands needs separately authorized
planning, engineering qualification and a complete approved live run card;
this assignment proposes no executable live procedure.
