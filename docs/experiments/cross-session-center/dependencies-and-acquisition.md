This map closes the current finite static leads at their actual information
boundaries. It does not claim every alias/derived writer in the executable has
been enumerated. Following additional allocator internals or a complete terrain
asset decoder cannot recover an unrecorded historical selected edge/flag/epoch.
An original deterministic re-decode reproduces the available bits and preserves
the missing fields as missing. Causal hypotheses remain observationally compatible:
an unrecorded conversion, an unrecorded replacement, or a different activation
epoch can produce the measured XYZ; timing intervention can alter those paths.
No arithmetic fit chooses among them.

The independent specialist challenged broad-writer-first acquisition and required
the old actual operation and trigger. Independent reviewer challenges and final
disposition are retained in [review](review.md). No proposed observation is built,
deployed, executed or treated as permission here. The external debugger path
remains closed. A future brief must specify finite actions, exact build/sample,
hook effects and ABI, protected saves, disk/integrity safeguards, flushing,
retirement/exit policy and explicit acceptance criteria; it must not impose an
arbitrary elapsed/event quota or quietly replace the scientific question.
