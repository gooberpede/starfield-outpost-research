[Searchable population](population.csv), [skill catalogue](catalogue.csv), [condition/entry metadata](entries.csv), [inheritance](inheritance.csv). Counts describe this installed main-game definition census, not actual recruited actors in a save.

The fresh main ESM walk contains 16,108 selected NPC/PERK/SPEL/MGEF/KYWD/AVIF records, including every 502 PERK. CR1's 56 category/template leads are preserved rather than relabelled 56 recruits. The qualified ordinary human subset has 28 named bases and 14 generic specialist archetypes. ## Recruitment qualification

Named crew bases combine category evidence with attached elite startup/quest or companion-role scripts; [inheritance.csv](inheritance.csv) records the actual attachment class. Elite NPC VMAD binds its own recruitment quest; the established [CR1](../crew/report.md) role/recruitment PEX implements availability and assignment. Main companions follow companion-role machinery. This establishes potential story-conditioned recruitment, not that every actor is available in every universe or at every stage. Names/keywords/skill possession alone do not recruit an actor.

Generic root `Crew_LvlCrew_Any 26f937` selects the installed `LCharCrew_Any 26f938`: fourteen LVLO entries positively enumerate the same fourteen specialist bases, including Security, Payload, Fusion Systems, Engineering and Management. This is a positive role population source, not a text search for attractive skills. Their generic role/template and hiring fragments are established in CR1. Level-list/template selection can create differing runtime actors; a historical generic's exact chosen base remains UNKNOWN unless its own selected template data are decoded.

Cora, Matteo, alternate/no-appearance story bodies, the duplicated Barrett power body and generic template-only records remain separate preserved leads, not asserted ordinary recruits. Their known direct skills are either already represented or helper data; no unique unseen effect is silently dropped. Player7, Walter Stroud, security/story actors and autopilot actors carrying crew-flagged perks are separately inventoried in `noncandidate-crew-perks01.json`; possession of such a flag does not qualify them for recruitment. Robin Swiss/story actors are not automatically ordinary player crew. DLC/Creation/mod recruits and overrides are outside the installed **main ESM** census, not claimed absent.

Five robot NPCs have direct positive COBJ CNAM construction recipes in `robot-recipes01.json`: Garden 04297c, Sanitation 0bd67e, Model A01/02/03 at 1f416e/1d9250/1d924e. Model A03 is a real definition holder of **outpost** Fusion 20d24f. This corrects an initial human-only acquisition scope before completion; no claim of “no holder anywhere” is retained. Model A01/02 carry Engineering/Management respectively. Construction/auto-assignment and actual eligible E2 membership are still different conditions.

## Template inheritance, rather than a union of ancestor perks

Native loader 1405d2590 reads the 60-byte TPTA array as 15 separate form slots. Actor template selection 1405d2d90/1405d3ad0 resolves each enabled part, including leveled choices, then 1405d4a00 assembles the effective NPC. The TESNPC override at vtable 144bd5f88+58 (`140cd77d0`, n024) explicitly selects **part 3**, tests template-use **bit 3** and merges both inherited spell identities and perk/rank rows, deduplicating existing perk identities. Its perk destination is NPC+248, count+250/data+258. A stats-template pointer is not an instruction to union all template perks.

All 28 qualified named bases and 14 specialist bases have **bit 3 clear** in their 16-byte ACBS (template mask at+14). Their direct PRKR lists therefore are not augmented by the balance template's Piloting/test-gas perks through this inheritance branch. The template 12fdde's test perk 26fe1a really exists and would halve gas duration if active; it is preserved in the catalogue rather than omitted or attributed to every named companion. The full ancestral union was tested as a lead and rejected as an inheritance rule by this native flag/part trace.

For the generic spawn root 26f937, bit 3 is set and TPTA slot 3 selects 26f938, so the chosen specialist's direct rank 1 perk can be inherited. Source choices and resulting effective ranks must be qualified per actual actor. Model A02/03 have bit 3 clear and their own direct Management/Fusion entries; their other template parts point to Model A01. Model A01 has its own Engineering entry and enabled spell/perk inheritance from 09b33e; that ancestor does not add another crew skill in the full NPC census. Garden/Sanitation have their own direct crew entries and explicit OutpostActorScript AssignAsCrewOnCreation=true. Robot assignment follows the existing [E1 script bridge](../inorganic-extractors/stacking-matrix.md); actual runtime assignment/eligibility is not inferred merely from construction.

## Grants, displayed ranks and scope of completeness

Ten fully closed installed compiled quest fragments contribute 40 actual AddPerk operations. Their property variables are resolved through each object's auto-property binding to installed QUST VMAD FormIDs. This prevents a source property name or unused property from becoming a fictitious skill. Lin/Heller, Vasco, Moara, Mathis, Amelia, Autumn, Erick, Rafael and Hadrian have conditional stage-specific grants. Erick's later Piloting grant is recorded separately from initial grants. Repeated grants increment/clamp a rank under the established native policy; a count of three calls is an intended rank 3 from no prior grant, not a universal assertion about historical actor state.

The table lists every distinct skill found in these qualified direct/conditional grants. It records actual definition/grant ranks separately from all defined entry ranks, Crew screen eligibility separately from the census, role/environment separately from effect mechanism, and actual saved ranks separately in [saved-ranks.csv](saved-ranks.csv). Companion_BasicPerk is positively retained as a non-UI internal helper, not silently hidden by flag filtering or counted as a named crew skill. Its effects remain in the entry table. No source description is substituted for its actual conditions.

Several crew forms have no native effect entries. Their role metadata can coexist with configured gifts/dialogue and does not grant the corresponding player skill. This is complete observed skill coverage within the qualified main-game population/grant routes, with story/variant/override and actual-runtime limits explicit. It is not a proof that no script anywhere can later grant another perk, or that a current loaded actor exactly matches the base record.
