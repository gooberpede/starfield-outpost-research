## Possession, display and effect registration are different

CR1's [screen trace](../crew/screen.md) supplies `14153dc70 → 14153e290 → 14153d900`: base/additional possessed perks, root flag8 display admission, then current-rank lookup. It does not evaluate A1, DB, AA or damage. A root crew flag does not create a perk entry. `Crew_Geology 21b8cc` has root DATA but **no PRKE**; Heller's compiled quest grants that form, not player Geology. The separate player form is not substituted by display-name equality.

Normal AddPerk `141954810` (n011) updates an actor's per-perk rank storage through `1412b5c60` (n004), then sends old/new ranks through `1418c4f00 → 1418c50a0 → 140ca3910` (n005/n006). The latter walks entries: `entry+8+1 == old` calls the entry method at vtable byte offset `0x70`; matching new rank
calls the method at vtable byte offset `0x68`. BGSEntryPointPerkEntry's vtable `144bd2870` binds these to `140ca1bd0`/`140ca1a20` (n004/n005). Registered type 2 entries occupy per-actor entry-point buckets; removal erases the exact pointer. Selected rank and all defined ranks are distinct. [E1](../inorganic-extractors/stacking-native.md) establishes priority ordering and normal rank replacement; CR2 reuses that result.

`140c93cf0` (n001) checks actor, entry-point bounds and actor bucket availability, constructs condition context, copies active entries and evaluates each passing entry before dispatching its operation. Crew environment handling inspects the first bucket entry's parent root flag/category, with player bypass. Nonzero category resolves the actor's CrewAssignment: ship base type41/flag gives1; Workshop identity plus its keyed owner gives2. Missing/mismatched environment skips the bucket's effect evaluation. Category0 does not impose that assignment gate. Do not generalize this implementation into an independently evaluated environment test on every hypothetical mixed-category entry in a modded bucket.

Entry condition groups (`PRKC`) select operands assembled from actor plus the consumer's context arguments. HasKeyword function0230 is the established native keyword condition; GetBaseValue0115/PowerGenerated and GetPerkRank01fb/explicit Player references have different operands. [Entries](entries.csv) preserves numeric groups, functions, comparison flags, parameters and operations. Text descriptions do not override these gates.

## A1: per-actor producer timing

End-to-end positive example: generic Management Specialist NPC `02fdf7` directly grants `21b8c3` rank 1; Lin's stage 1 compiled quest grants that perk three times. Normal assignment `1404076b0` (n001) appends one actor handle to owner CrewExtraData E2 and establishes CrewAssignment. The scheduler `140f7ef20 → 140f7bd20` obtains the Workshop's E2, resolves handles, skips virtual+868 true and requires positive Health. It passes each eligible actor, the **producer base** and an in/out duration to A1 through `140c94060 → 140c93cf0`.

The Management entry's category2 passes only with an outpost assignment. Its entry conditions accept the installed gas/liquid/solid/organic/manufactured producer keywords. Operation3 is `140c94430` (n004): it multiplies the pointed duration by the selected scalar. Another actor is evaluated against the same accumulating float; no highest-actor selector occurs in this loop. The player is dispatched afterward. Effective positive HarvesterRateMult then multiplies the timer during scheduler reset. Input/power/halt gates, due-cycle arithmetic, recipe removal and receiving remain separate [E1/E2/E3 stages](processes.md).

Contrasting Engineering has A1 too, but only manufactured keyword23ceb6. Garden/Sanitation robot entries have their own organic/inorganic selectors. Thus neither common A1 dispatch nor the word “production” proves a matching entry. Fresh fueled-marker `219523` in `focused01.json` has none of299548/299549/29954a/2c5a95/23ceb6; ordinary crew Management and Engineering therefore fail the keyword groups there. An unconditional player Guide entry is different. Existing [C3 fuel](../cargo-links/fuel/native.md) supplies the five-unit recipe and108-second base; CR2 resolves the ordinary crew applicability boundary.

## DB: additive power on each generator

`140669880 → 140f3dd50` (n002) is the [P1](../power/fuel.md) generation path. It uses positive base generation and preliminary base+AV-modifier total, linked distinct Workshop and E2. Each resolved, eligible, positive-Health crew actor is dispatched with entry DB and the generator reference; there is no extra player evaluation. The dispatcher operation 2 handler `140c94350` (n004) performs float addition.

`Crew_Outpost_AneutronicFusion 20d24f`, physical ESM 1290039702, is the sole DB owner in the 502 main PERKs. All three entries are **PRKE rank 0 (displayed selected rank 1)**, not three selectable ranks. Condition group 1 reads **base** PowerGenerated AV 032e on the supplied generator, not its modified total. Registration instruction 1400caa47 stores 1115 (condition 0115 plus console 1000), names GetBaseValue/GetBaseAV, and links condition callback 140be3fb0; that callback invokes AV-owner virtual+18 (n027/n028/n029). Flags 80/60 implement below 6, at-least 6/below 12, and at-least 12 bands, yielding +1/+2/+3. Conditions inspect generator base AV state; accumulating the separate output float does not change that AV between actors. This permits additive per-actor contributions, subject to eligibility and actual possession. Model A03 NPC 1d924e directly grants this rank 1 perk and COBJ 1d924d constructs that NPC. No ordinary human holder is established by the qualified recruit grants. Rafael's VMAD property is positively present but unused by the fully decoded AddPerk body.

## Type1 abilities: ship Fusion and destination state

`Crew_AneutronicFusion 222f83`, physical ESM 1290073635, is category 1 and contains a type 1 selected-rank 1 ability `198bb6`. That SPEL links MGEF 198bb9 and AV 198bb8, SpaceshipReactorPower. Its numeric magnitude is 1. The player's Fusion `2c2c5a` has separate abilities/ranks; neither is the DB generator entry.

Assignment refresh `140408bb0 → 14040a230 → 14040a9d0` (n001/n002) checks the actor's keyed destination, destination environment and selected rank. The inner path specifically requires entry virtual+20 ==1. It compares other crew with the same perk/rank through `14040a840`; equal/higher competing grants suppress application, and a stronger replacement removes the old matching effect. Eligible ability categories are routed to destination effect work through `1418c3600`, using the destination reference as target. Type2 timer entries fail this type 1 branch. Removal has a separate selected-entry walk `14040a360 → 1404094b0` (n013 and follow-up locators); assignment-driven ability state is not the same as the next A1/DB call.

An outpost destination's environment2 does not admit the ordinary Fusion category 1 ability. The ability modifies its specific ship reactor AV, while P1's generator consumer reads PowerGenerated032e and DB. Both the admission and the eventual consumed value differ. General ship reactor allocation and all player/crew ability combinations are outside this bounded contrast.

## AA: ship capacity, not a CONT CarryWeight aura

`14211b700` (n002) reads effective ship CarryWeight plus shielded capacity, obtains **that ship's** E2 and selected perk actor through `1421230a0`, then calls `140409f60(AA, actor, E2, capacity*)`. `1404095c0` (n001) resolves eligible crew, tests active entries/conditions and selects the strictly highest qualifying entry rank. It subsequently evaluates all passing entries of that selected actor, then the separately supplied actor. It does not combine every crew actor or maximize the final float. Tied ranks retain the earlier actor.

`1421230a0` selects Player for player-base/current-home-ship paths; otherwise `1421159b0 → 14212d230` resolves the ship-associated actor from ship management. Another qualifying actor predicate can redirect to Player. Failed resolution need not supply player perks. None of these paths reads a linked outpost's crew list. Crew Payloads 222f84 provides AA operation 3 factor 1.1 at every defined selected rank; Player Payloads 143b6b has 1.1/1.2/1.3/1.5 and its own rank conditions.

Dedicated CONT and cargo bay capacity use their own effective CarryWeight interface `140b6ce20`; [S1](../storage/capacity-native.md) and [C2 transfer](../cargo-links/transport/inventory.md) separate CONT from spaceship type41. Ship capacity effects can change a qualifying ship hold; an assigned outpost Payload specialist is not automatically in that ship's E2. Cargo-link player Management buff0ce860 is a distinct conditional AA definition; the report does not infer its active ownership from the existence of a PERK record.

## Combat and non-effect role metadata

Pistol perk 27df98 has type 2 entry 23/operation 3 with pistol keyword 04a0a0 and conditions on player Isolation perk 2c890d. The damage path `141b30b30` (n013), instructions `141b30e94..141b30eb4`, supplies the attacking actor, weapon/instance context and target to `140409f80`. That wrapper can add the actor's current ship E2 through `142116840`, then evaluates the actor; it has no outpost-owner E2 walk. The broader damage pipeline has multiple channels/dispatches: do not equate one scalar with the final entire attack. Supplied context must satisfy the entry conditions.

An outpost security specialist can benefit in its own qualifying combat; assignment does not transfer that actor's pistol perk to each turret. The same distinction applies to other actor-local combat/health/carry abilities in the catalogue. Comprehensive attacks/turrets remain deferred. Geology, Chemistry, Botany, Medicine, Gastronomy, Scavenging and Theft crew forms have no PRKE entries. Their display/role identity is not proof of player crafting/scanning/harvesting effects. Installed COM_CREW_GiveItemActorScript uses configured GiveItemList and availability/cooldown conditions to add gifts to Player; its inspected body does not multiply Workshop production. Full unrelated dialogue/gift economies are not audited.
