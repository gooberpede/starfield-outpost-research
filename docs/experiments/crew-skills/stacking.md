# Combination, rank precedence and numerical examples

[Native paths](native.md), [entries](entries.csv), [process scope](processes.md). Numbers below are conditional calculations from installed definitions, not measured running outpost values. Native binary32 operations round each step; printed decimal factors are convenient representations of the stored scalars.

| Mechanism | Different actors / skills | Rank, duplicate and removal policy | Player interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 timer | Each eligible actor's passing active entries multiply the current duration. Management and Engineering both contribute on manufacturing; Engineering fails ordinary organic/inorganic | Normal assignment deduplicates actor handles, not perk identities across actors. Rank change removes exact old entries and registers selected new entries. One repeated AddPerk advances/clamps rank; it is not an independent contributor | Player's active A1 entries are dispatched after E2 actors, with their own conditions |
| DB generator contribution | Each eligible assigned actor adds passing entry scalar to the shared base float; distinct actors can add even with same perk | Selected rank1 has three alternative power bands. These are not ranks1/2/3 or three cumulative bonuses. Reassignment removes actor from old E2 before future reads | This power loop has no extra player dispatch; player ship Fusion has a separate type1 mechanism |
| AA ship capacity | One crew actor chosen by highest passing entry rank, then all passing entries of that actor are evaluated. It is not max final capacity or one winner per distinct perk | First equally ranked qualifying actor is retained by strict rank comparison; actor-list order matters for unequal entries at tied rank. Normal rank transition still replaces entries | Selected ship owner/current player context is evaluated afterward; caller chooses that actor, not universally GetPlayer |
| Type1 destination ability | Assignment/refresh inspects selected-rank crew abilities; competing same-perk rank suppresses lower/equal application, higher replaces prior matching destination effect | Ability removal/reapplication is stateful and may queue magic-effect work. This is a different branch from type2 A1/DB entries | Player-owned ship abilities and their effect conditions are distinct from crew grants; no blanket sum/max law across unrelated abilities |
| Actor combat | Passing actor/weapon/target entries modify damage channels, with ship wrapper where called; no all-outpost-crew fold | Current selected-rank entries and conditions govern each damage evaluation | Player Isolation conditions can select different crew combat factors. Do not add all rank definitions or extrapolate to outpost turrets |

Timer examples:

1. Two rank1 Management actors on ordinary inorganic: `.95 × .95 = .9025` of duration. Adding player Outpost Management rank4 gives `.45125`. Rate reciprocals are approximately 1.10803 and 2.21607 of the otherwise identical baseline, **not** 9.75% and 54.875% rate increases.
2. Real HIST-0255 Lin/Heller saved ranks, assuming both pass current evaluation on a fabricated resource: `.87 × .77 = .6699`. On inorganic, Heller's Engineering fails, so `.87` remains. On organic the same Engineering exclusion holds. Actual selected facility, power/inputs, Health and elapsed state are still required.
3. One Management rank1 actor changing to rank3 contributes `.87`, not `.95 × .91 × .87`. Two actors at ranks1/3 give `.8265`. With player rank4 Management on inorganic: `.95 × .87 × .5 = .41325`. Player Special Projects rank4 can further multiply by .95 where its conditions pass; magazine rank4/5 contributes one selected .95 entry, not both historical ranks.
4. Ordinary fuel marker: the crew Management/Engineering family tests fail, so these examples cannot shorten its 108-second base cycle. A selected unconditional Guide .95 entry gives 102.6 seconds before a positive effective HarvesterRateMult; five He-3 per accepted cycle remains five. Faster cycling can consume *more* He-3 over a fixed eligible elapsed interval.

Power example, **conditional on actually possessing the separate outpost perk**: at base PowerGenerated band12+, two eligible actors add3+3. Base20 with zero AV modifiers gives26. A different generator with base6-to-below12 gets2 per actor. Base20 with AV modifier -10 still selects3 per actor: two give `20 + 3 + 3 - 10 = 16`; band selection does not use the modified10. This is per generator, not one +3 pool shared by all generators. Base20 with an AV modifier −20 gives preliminary zero and bypasses the crew loop; no bonus revives that suppressed generator.

Capacity example: eligible ship crew Payloads rank1 and rank2 choose rank2; its installed scalar is still1.1. With a selected player Payloads rank4 entry passing, the conditional capacity multiplier is `1.1 × 1.5 = 1.65`, not `1.1² × 1.5`. A cargo-link ship's actual owner/crew context and separate cargo-link buff must be established before assigning this example to it. Dedicated storage never enters this AA ship helper.

Duplicate malformed/restored E2 lists, overlapping external grants, concurrency during rank replacement and altered plugins are exceptions outside normal insertion/settled-transition proofs. No atomic snapshot across all actor rank removals/additions or ability effects is established. Dynamic per-call calculations need no fictitious universal cached “crew bonus”; scheduler countdowns and applied abilities have their own persistent/transient state.
