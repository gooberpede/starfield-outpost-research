## Implemented products and outcomes

[Builder](../../../research/resource-standalone/builder.py) reads pinned installed
main definitions, EXE constants and PlanetData maps, recomputes all planetary
selection dependencies, then projects the three supported tiles. It writes
the two local corpora, lineage/identity manifest and a flat five-module code
bundle. No historical native answer or expected region enters preparation.

[Predictor](../../../research/resource-standalone/predictor.py) consumes only that
corpus and the explicit chart/descriptor/XYZ/radius/upper request. It resolves
sources, rebuilds seed keys, generates every selected competing resource in native
order, constructs bounds and derives geometric availability and callback results.
The unchanged owned arithmetic remains the numeric reference. Different
languages can implement the [specified pipeline](algorithm.md) and
[portable format](corpus.md) without the game or extractor at inference.

At Feynman, ordinary geometric presence is supported for Nickel, Cobalt, Copper
and Ionic Liquids; Fluorine is ABSENT_WITHIN_PROVEN_SCOPE for the modeled
footprint. At Carinae, Iron, Alkanes and Rothicite are PRESENT. These are
conditional ordinary-vein conclusions. They make no final UI or Water absence
claim. Point-center membership is recorded separately: all Feynman paths miss
the exact query center, while one Carinae Rothicite path contains its center.
Area presence and point membership answer different questions.

There are zero missing native controls. The remaining 99 Feynman and 1323 Carinae
generated paths lack a witness in this seven-control set and remain UNVERIFIED.
They are not declared native extras, false positives, connected deposits or
placed extractor identities. Both site responses explicitly retain UNKNOWN
native census and historical epoch even when modeled source enumeration closes.

Scientific outcome is positive bounded standalone reconstruction, extending the
previous conditional geometry without changing frozen predictions. Engineering
evidence includes four isolated inference runs, variant equality, ten group
removal/perturbation experiments, repeated deterministic builds and 96 passing
regressions. Scientific negatives and earlier engineering failures remain in
[validation](validation.md) and ignored immutable receipts. Target survival,
restoration and live cleanup are not applicable: no target operated.

Publication outcome is NOT CLEARED. The schema/algorithm and authored research
are the principal future publication outputs; code publication is undecided.
Actual extracted payloads stay ignored. [Rights and prospective plan](rights.md)
separate that decision from scientific confidence. No new permission issue,
third-party incorporation, contact or external disclosure occurred.
