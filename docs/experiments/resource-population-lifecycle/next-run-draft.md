## Bounded question and outcomes

For Save52's second Feynman V Workshop, is source
`0:37536:20928:0029d20e` materially populated before an independently qualified
native load-completion event, or only later? Is a later consumer reading that
same population lifetime? Neither answer alone decides whether geometry is saved.

| Observed sequence | Supported outcome |
|---|---|
| Qualified construction finishes between load begin/end, joined to target source and later consumer with uninterrupted lifetime evidence | Load-time construction and that construction-to-consumption ancestry, for this new run only. |
| Coherent selected-source holder exists at load end, but its origin was not observed | Presence at load end only; origin and serialization remain UNKNOWN. |
| Qualified target absence at load end, later bound miss/construction/consume | Later construction in this run; completeness of selected-source lookup and source-index readiness must be demonstrated. |
| Target source cannot be mapped, lock/lifetime unsafe, incomplete stage or observation gap | INCOMPLETE for timing/ancestry; preserve useful independent consumer or constructor observations. |
| A joined stream loader restores the actual region arrays before consumer use | Evidence for geometry restoration; require stream provenance and distinguish stored recipe or deterministic reconstruction. This extra question needs its own approved scope. |

## Required event and identity qualification

Before live approval, identify and statically qualify actual save-load begin/end
boundaries. Menu appearance, operator readiness, Workshop restoration and arrival
are not substitutes. Bind the event to the protected Save52 input and one process.
If the boundary cannot be qualified, this design cannot decide load timing.

Use exact Workshop bytes `b0e207c2dee2c1b6`, owner/body `0x0005e0de`, qualified
associated files and native source fields to map the selected geographic label.
Record measured module base, source/index/manager lifetimes, full source pointer,
source `+0x68`, current mode/descriptor, map branch/slot/holder and collection
identities. Pointer equality, hashes, ordinals and timestamps alone cannot create
a lifetime join. Observer tokens must be labelled side records, not engine fields.

Observe birth/insertion, completed construction or early exit, provider return,
reset/destruction/replacement and actual selected consumption, or supply an
independently proven fence covering every relevant alias. A missed lifecycle
event invalidates ancestry. D5B's rotating hardware schedule cannot supply that
proof; a new design must resolve slot availability and scheduling loss explicitly.
Do not silently extend its four-slot collector or call equal bytes an epoch.

At construction retain original signed Int2, converted geographic argument,
BIOM/component readiness, sampled mask, effective ordered descriptors/weights,
source frame and complete relevant output geometry. At consumption retain
query/scalars, actual selected collection, ACCEPT edge and same-evaluation
transfer. Capture before travel as well as at consumption. Natural absence must
be distinguished from an uninitialized source index or incomplete read.

## Qualification, intervention and protection gates

First qualify the observation mechanism on original disposable fixtures: pre-load
presence, load-time construction, lazy construction, early prerequisite exit,
reset/replacement, same-address reuse, nested/concurrent calls, unavailable source,
truncated arrays, event loss and complete evidence surviving post-flush exit.
Synthetic success establishes engineering behavior only. It cannot establish
native source epochs, field meanings or that retail timing matches a fixture.

The approved future card must specify executable/instruction/tool pins, owner and
alias protection, exact hooks/breakpoints and register effects, finite action
scope, actual memory/queue bounds, durable flush/ACK and STOP semantics, save
classification/backups, cleanup and crash/exit recovery. Preserve the
40,000,000,000-byte caller-available guard on both C: and D:. No arbitrary healthy
time/event/volume cutoff follows from this design. Observer attachment, pauses,
breakpoints or hooks affect execution and must be disclosed as intervention.

No forced reset, memory/register patch, console regeneration, source/save edit,
instrument installation, game launch, attachment, fallback site or gameplay action
is authorized here. Originals, frozen alternatives and all ten geometry controls
remain protected. A separately approved one-case experiment should stop after
its finite useful observations, explicit operator STOP or an actual integrity,
identity, worker or storage failure under the approved recovery policy. A
pre-evidence crash is failure; persisted complete evidence and target survival
remain separate outcomes.
