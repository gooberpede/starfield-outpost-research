## Editorial and delivery procedure

Manual review covers every new Markdown file, all CSV headings/labels and
qualifications, all six Python modules' explanatory text, and the six changed
index/state/source/permission/credit documents. The supplied brief is included
unchanged and reviewed as governing input. Hashes/symbols receive identity checks.
Check distinctions between source/site and historical epoch; ordinary/Special/
atmospheric Water; multipliers/chances; native paths/visible deposits; exact
geometry controls and their limited source identity. The screen alone is not
acceptance. No exception is claimed to avoid independent editorial review.

`package.py --label review01` exports complete/task-only diffs under
`.local-work/diffs/rg2-d5a-review01-*.diff`, including untracked text without
staging. Complete includes the supplied unchanged brief; task-only excludes it.
`review01/package.json`, `changed-files.json`, `exclusions.json` and
`git-status-short.txt` retain patch hashes, byte/path inventory, forward/reverse
reconstruction, links, whitespace and unchanged HEAD/index. Raw inputs, outputs,
protected saves, corpora, ZIP/images, native exports, analysis databases and
scratch trees are excluded and remain local. Later review revisions require
new numbered packages and relevant independent review.
