## Modifier eligibility checked for the organic context

Fresh main-ESM PERK fields in inventory02 corroborate the earlier 502-PERK
census. Interpret HasKeyword conditions using the [E1 condition/native binding](../inorganic-extractors/production-modifiers.md).
Organic facility bases actually carry `002c5a95`, so the following eligible
definition entries reach the shared timer only when selected on a qualifying
actor/player and their other predicates pass. Factors multiply duration.

| Source | Organic applicability on inspected entries |
|---|---|
| Crew Outpost Management `0021b8c3` | Organic is one of its OR keyword alternatives; selected ranks 1/2/3 contribute .95/.91/.87 |
| Garden minibot perk `0004297e` | Organic keyword required; ranks 1/2/3 contribute .91/.83/.77. NPC `0004297c` has PRKR rank 1; actual supplied-list inclusion still required |
| Constellation Guide `00208e92` | A1 .95 entries at native ranks 4/5 have no keyword condition; ordinary selected-rank replacement applies, not both ranks merely because both exist |
| Survey production boost `0030b589` | Some .95 entries include organic; other survey/acquisition conditions remain qualified. Do not assume every surveyed planet contributes |
| Player Outpost Management `0023826f` rank 4 | Its .5 A1 entry lists only gas/liquid/solid; **do not transfer the E1 .5 bonus to organic** |
| Special Projects `0004ce2d` rank 4 | Its .95 A1 entry lists only gas/liquid/solid; excluded from organic under these entries |
| Crew Engineering `0021b8c8`; Sanitation `0004297f` | Manufactured / inorganic conditions respectively; not organic timer entries |

E1's handle-unique assignment, ordinary rank replacement and per-actor composition
are shared consumer mechanisms. They do not prove that a particular robot is
assigned or that every listed bonus is active. Organic inputs do not create a
new bonus formula; quantity, effective duration, eligibility and realization stay
separate. Horticulture/Domestication research gates construction recipes; no new
direct research-completion timer grant is established. Botany/Zoology and scanning
must be read through the selector's knowledge contract, not guessed from these
production timer perks.

## Future shared interfaces

The organic category shares builder menu recipe identity, resource AV selection,
input traversal, cycle capping and consumption with neighboring builder machinery.
These are interface discoveries, not a fabricator study. D1 receives no new
incident evidence or hypothesis disposition. After manufactured production,
storage, local transfers and cargo links each have their own completed maps,
assess whether all native contracts together suffice to follow a multi-planet,
multi-system chain. That integrated research sufficiency test remains a roadmap
question; no solver, importer, mod or logistics implementation is delivered here.
