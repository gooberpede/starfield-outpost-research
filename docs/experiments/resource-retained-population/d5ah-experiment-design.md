# D5AH future geometry and extractor comparison

**DESIGN ONLY — NOT AUTHORIZED for implementation, deployment or execution.**
Stage A identifies the missing observation: complete populations on both sides
of a verified native lifetime transition, joined independently to an established
extractor's restored identity and production state. B1/B2 and fixed-input hosts
cannot supply that historical comparison. The proposed contrast below tests a
new episode; it cannot recover missing B2 fields.

Before approving a run, resolve that saved reference into the loaded instance,
verify actual Workshop owner, effective base/resource properties, power/operating
conditions and output capacity, and confirm it is an appropriate ordinary
natural extractor. Refuse the comparison if identity or owner is ambiguous;
do not silently substitute a new placement. Reconcile the historical saved

frame. A saved local XYZ candidate is not an authoritative runtime transform.

## Smallest useful episode

1. From the verified working copy, reach the named existing outpost without
   changing its placed objects. Record starting world/component/descriptor,
   source allocation and token, full effective input contract, complete
   population A, candidate transform and producer resource association.
2. Observe one operator-controlled departure and direct return. Record the
   actual coverage shift, retirement, Grid clear and broad-reset boundaries;
   do not infer the path from travel UI or an observer epoch increment.
3. At return, capture complete population B under the same verified native
   ownership/frame contract, source/holder/construction lineage and restored
   extractor state. Independently measure its producer eligibility, resource-keyed
   production/insertion and destination inventory over a declared native
   game-time interval, with power,
   capacity, recipe/resource and unloaded catch-up conditions recorded.

One departure/return can distinguish reuse from fresh construction only if
the required lifetime and construction witness is actually captured. If it
retains the holder, it does not test reconstruction. The smallest justified
additional contrast is a separately approved ordinary full-load comparison
from the **same immutable working-copy starting bytes**, followed by the same
arrival. Use the existing native full-load reset paths rather than forcing a
reset or patching a visited store. This is a proposed conditional contrast,
not authorization for automatic replay, retries or repeated sampling until a
preferred outcome appears. Whether full-load reliably supplies the required
new construction must itself be observed.

## Measurements and stable ownership

| Measurement | Required ground truth and safety contract |
|---|---|
| Starting-state equivalence | Save hash/size, EXE/content/plugin/config identities, owner/component/descriptor, effective remapping and settings. Record changed consequential inputs rather than calling the run equal-input. |
| Source and construction lineage | Birth/publication and retirement IDs, copied geographic/content identity and token, protected map decision, holder insertion, generator entry/return and reset/clear records. Pointer equality is insufficient because allocations can be reused. |
| Complete geometry | All source contributions relevant to the outpost and chosen point, all represented resource keys/regions/ordered points, header metadata and frame. Include U22–U24 or prove their irrelevance for the actual point; U25 alone is not a complete outpost census. |
| Copy validity | Read only while the verified native reader/owner lease protects the exact holder/collections. Validate count/capacity/pointer bounds, retain/copy obligations, successful completion, generation stage, snapshot coherence and all scalar lengths before publishing the snapshot. An empty/partial copy is an invalid measurement, not changed geometry. |
| Effective generation inputs | Original query, raster byte/readiness, source origin/clip, owner/cell seed keys, ordered definitions/weights/DNAM, component/extra-pass applicability and preparation dependencies. Saved PCM seed alone is insufficient. |
| Extractor continuity | Exact loaded reference/base, Workshop owner and resource-map key; serialized attachment/property and restored producer registration, eligibility failures and power/capacity. Correlate recipe/product insertion with the producer and actual destination selected by `0x140f7d510`; record that receiver's inventory and other producers/transfers/consumption. The machine's own inventory or an unexplained net item delta is insufficient. Keep association, production and current point eligibility separate. |
| Time and transition | Native game-time interval, loaded/unloaded/catch-up state, consumer refresh ordering, input/output flush/ACK and loss/error status. An arrival report is a bounded operator observation, not a native transition witness. |

Any proposed instrumentation must first prove the actual reader/owner acquisition
and release edges offline. Do not copy borrowed raw source pointers after their
lease ends, infer a source lease from a Grid reference, wait on I/O under a
native lock, or deep-copy an unbounded collection in a hooked hot path. Design
an ownership-safe capture/publication mechanism and qualify memory/queue bounds,
callback latency, reentrancy, contention, failure reporting and lossless flush.
Performance acceptance must be justified by the chosen mechanism, without an
arbitrary scientific duration/event quota. Instrumentation affects execution;
no intervention-free claim is appropriate.

Existing schema 4 supplies identity/lifetime joins, reset/Grid events, map
decisions, direct sample and visited store. It lacks complete geometry and
the independent extractor production/restore observations required here.
Observer epoch is a recorder domain, not a native generation counter. Precise
future additions are full owned population snapshots plus construction/holder
lineage and the placed producer observations above. No observer source or
binary is changed by D5AH. Extra scheduling clocks are necessary only if a
winner-causality claim is included in a separately approved scope.

## Falsifiable outcomes and controls

| Competing explanation | Observation that supports it | Observation that falsifies it for this contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Retained original geometry | Continuous qualified holder/collection lineage, no applicable erase/reset, equal complete A/B. | Proven new construction after owner invalidation; equal pointers alone cannot rescue retention. |
| Independently reconstructed identical geometry | Positive new holder/generator witness after invalidation; complete A/B equal in the same verified frame with equivalent effective inputs. | Complete valid A/B differ, or lineage shows reuse rather than reconstruction. |
| Independently reconstructed different geometry | Positive new construction and valid complete A/B differ. Record whether effective inputs also differ. | Valid complete A/B equal for this episode. This does not establish universal equality. |
| Extractor association independent of current point geometry | Same restored producer resource key and valid production delta while a separately verified current point query excludes that resource. | Qualified production path instead requires the current point query and fails specifically on that spatial condition. No production delta alone is inconclusive. |

Use a within-episode no-transition reread as the snapshot/coherence control,
and inspect the unchanged source/holder lineage before interpreting a changed
snapshot. Preserve all resource identities and full points; resource names,
mask, screenshots and Workshop area lists are insufficient. A nearby free-space
placement query may test present spatial eligibility without placing or moving
an object, if separately approved; it cannot stand in for the established
producer. A stopped extractor with full inventory, no power or no elapsed
production time is ambiguous, not falsification of persistence.

If the ordinary transition never changes geometry, it can resolve retention
or identical reconstruction for that contrast but cannot positively test
continuity under differing geometry. Do not force different geometry or change
the protected save. Report that branch as unobserved and assess the smallest
new authorized contrast from the captured effective inputs.

## Future authorization boundary

A future card must pin the actual build, working copy/backup, observation scope,
owner/lifetime mechanism, interventions, allowed operator transitions, stop and
exit/recovery policy, free-space floor of 40,000,000,000 caller-available bytes
on both C: and D:, protected-input pre/post audits and evidence flush/ACK.
Stop on identity/guard failure, evidence corruption, unexpected protected-input
change, worker failure or explicit operator STOP. A delayed operator action or
routine poll alone is not a stop condition. No save resave/restoration, observer
modification/deployment, launch, attachment or live GO follows from this design.
