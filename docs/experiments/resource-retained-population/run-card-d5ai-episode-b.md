## Discrimination and limits

| Result | Required evidence | Meaning and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Rebuilt identical | Independent new process and positively captured source/population construction history; full qualified A0/B0 normalized bytes equal in the same frame. | Equal content in this pair despite distinct lifetime. No universal reproducibility or schedule identity. |
| Rebuilt different | Same qualified new-lifetime history and complete A0/B0 bytes differ. Preserve every differing field, effective input change and native quality. | Concrete difference in this pair. It does not assign the cause to a mask or scheduler without corresponding input evidence. |
| Unavailable | Complete actual consumer scan proves expected population absent or unexposed in the correct frame. | Scoped boundary result. External retained allocations, other-source contributions and cached UI are separate. |
| Inconclusive | Missing construction/identity/frame, cached refresh/no scan, partial/malformed output, ambiguity, relevant loss or failed control. | Preserve narrower observations. Neither equal resources nor missing records repairs the gap. |

New process/session, expected source/body/frame and completed map scan qualified?

  yes -> Population exposed?

cannot establish a long-run frequency, universal repeatability or GC-10's usual
retail winner. Correct prediction alone does not prove a mechanism; a responsibly
made wrong prediction is useful evidence, not failure of the research process.
