## Why the centre and radius were omitted

The **150-native-unit radius is known**. Drawing its circle is straightforward
once its centre is established in the plotted coordinates. The missing D5U
qualification was the outpost-to-plot coordinate join, not the radius itself.

D5U deliberately fixed each prospective source anchor at (0,0), with generator
origin (-200,-200), to compare generation contracts. That is not a declaration
that an actual outpost centre or beacon occupies (0,0). Its native geometry is
also not a translated replay of every historical outpost's generation frame:
frame-dependent seed operands can change the generated population.

Earlier [D2 research](../resource-save52/report.md#prediction-independence-and-portable-coverage)
already contains historical saved-site centre projections and conditional
150-unit research disks. The source matrix also retains descriptor and source
origin evidence. Therefore the earlier unqualified wording “centre UNKNOWN” was
too broad if read as saying the project has no centre evidence. What remains
UNKNOWN is the validated centre/beacon alignment **in these D5U plot contracts**.
No new save inspection was needed to establish that distinction.

A future reference overlay can use existing authenticated coordinate evidence
after explicitly deriving its translation, checking the native centre separately
from the beacon, and labelling the historical reference versus prospective
generated geometry. An actual outpost disk should then show the 150-unit radius
in the same transform, extending the view if necessary. U26 has no associated
outpost and must not receive a fabricated centre or circle.

This follow-up adds the requested names and corrects the explanation. It does
not claim that the coordinate join is impossible, absent from all evidence, or
already validated. No circle is added without that qualification.
