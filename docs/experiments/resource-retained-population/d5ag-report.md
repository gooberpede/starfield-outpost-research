## D5AG-Q1: terrain-to-resource authority

The ordinary chain has multiple kinds of authority. Descriptor/SFTR/SFPT and
content selection constrain the source allocation, ownership, extent, BIOM and
seed-cell frame. Source allocation deduplicates equal anchors within its Grid.
Focus, cell preparation and bounded LOD produce geographic requests with their
own original coordinates. Coordinate DB keys deduplicate requests; they do not
replace the original provider Int2 with a source anchor. The provider selects
contributors through the actual Grid, then converts its original query for
the generator's raster sample. Source-derived origin/clip and seed-cell operands
remain separate. No inspected anchor-deduplication or shared-transform edge
canonicalizes every request reaching one source.

Content preparation is material. Restored PCM placements flow into per-cell
preparation records and filtering maps; terrain blocks have real dependency,
publication and context-reader lifetimes. These constrain readiness and work,
without becoming a serialized resource population or an authoritative common
raster query. The integrated map records concrete producer/consumer paths and
negative controls, rather than excluding TerrainDB, LOD or PCM by name.

Q1 has a supported complete ordinary-path input/ownership map at the stated
producer/receiver boundaries. Historical effective values are distinct from
their mapped producers. The exact-final review matrix
governs whether the original completeness obligation is satisfied; no claim
extends to arbitrary external factories, content overrides or every engine path.

## D5AG-Q2: construction consistency and B2

Locality and task dependencies are real stabilizing candidates. Early
geographic registration can have an opportunity advantage; rank/serial, ready
dependencies, owner/peer claims and shared-pool progress affect which request
reaches the owner-wide protected map. Cold terrain waiting is not necessarily
passive: the resolved height wait can execute other queued work. Terrain
preparation therefore cannot be treated as a universal resource-construction
barrier or proof of a stable geographic winner.

An ambient terrain reader can nevertheless protect a resource invocation
cooperatively nested inside its height wait against another thread's ordinary
exclusive context shift. That is a concrete conditional lifetime stabilizer,
with separate same-thread recursion qualification. It neither elects a query
nor establishes that B2 executed in that context.

The prior original-native rival continuations and complete geometry controls
still provide a concrete counterexample to unconditional content-only winner
or geometry uniqueness under their fixed ordinary contract. They do not
estimate typical retail progress or disprove a normal statistical preference.
First protected miss is the local authority rule; it is not the upstream causal
explanation by itself.

B2 records `[4,-1]` unvisited at sequence 8006, `[5,0]` unvisited at 8020, then
the latter's miss 8091, sample `0x3` at 8094 and matching token store 8106;
the former reaches map hit 8339. Both checks precede their respective map
decisions in QPC time. The check is stamped after shared release and before
observer return/exclusive admission. The lock permits competing writer CAS and
incoming readers despite pending writers. Source retirement/reset does not
explain this particular reversal under the authenticated interval.

The strongest historical causal statement is that `[5,0]` returned from generation
and stored its visited token before `[4,-1]` reached its protected map hit,
despite the earlier visited stamp. Complete geometry was not recorded.
The actual delay before admission versus lock contention is not identifiable:
B2 lacks callback-return, pending-registration and ownership-acquisition events.
Terrain static paths cannot manufacture those clocks. Q2 remains BLOCKED for
the required historical/ordinary-execution distinction; known conditional
constraints and counterexamples are positive findings, not a generic race claim.

## D5AG-Q3: lifecycle consistency

The [transition model](d5ag-lifecycle-consistency.md) separates first cold
construction, aggregate/map reuse, visited bypass, partial retirement, broad
reset, restored PCM state and new generation. Ordinary source retirement can
erase a holder while leaving visitation; return can therefore bypass with no
new holder. Broad reset clears both stores and permits a new competition.
Full load restores descriptors and PCM inputs through separate serializers;
it does not restore old ProcGen pointers or represented region/point arrays.

This gives a conditional native explanation for retained identity and for
fresh reconstruction. Outpost/extractor point/area consumers use resident
geometry; their mapped paths do not establish a feedback pin preserving it
across reset. Existing production alone is not an oracle of later point geometry.
No actual gameplay bug, return failure or extractor displacement is asserted.

The specific complete population across a controlled retail departure/load/
return has not been captured. Neither Save52's serialized inputs nor B1/B2's
single-arrival sample/store fields identify those missing outputs and effective
inputs. Q3 remains BLOCKED for that empirical continuation; the compiled
retention/removal/reconstruction model is supported separately.

## D5AG-Q4: geometry and falsifiable explanation

The strongest supported model has three layers: stable source/preparation
inputs constrain eligible construction, actual admission chooses a query class,
and a successful generation is retained until its relevant lifetime ends.
There is no contradiction between apparently stable gameplay, conditional cached
reuse and multiple possible independent cold outcomes. The observations do not
select which mechanism explains every player's repeated return.

Two fresh native hosts each reproduce all three ordinary resources. Complete
mask-0 output has 74 regions/193 points; mask 3 has 78/180. Distinct supplied
queries `[4,1]` and `[5,0]` agree in every represented region/point under the
fixed contract; `[4,-1]` differs despite identical resource identities. Independent
planar arithmetic verifies every point/bound. Applicable Special high bits,
changed definitions/order, owner/frame seed keys, clipping and settings prevent
generalizing low-nibble equality alone. D5U preserves more-than-two-outcome
source evidence; a constant seed cannot cancel other effective differences.

These controls answer the fixed ordinary equivalence/difference question and
rigorously delimit broader claims. [Discriminators](d5ag-discriminators.md)
give predictions and exact observations for each surviving mechanism. Q4's
explanatory model is supported; retail repeatability remains the explicit Q2/Q3
and GC-31 gap, never a passing fixed-stage test.
