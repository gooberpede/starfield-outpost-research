## Equivalence contract

A complete population comparison includes every resource key, each region's
count and shape, ordered point coordinates and their native scalar bytes,
bounding data, and the coordinate frame. Comparing only resource membership,
aggregate counts, one deposit, a screenshot or a checksum of selected rows
cannot establish full geometry equality. If order is semantically normalized,
the normalization and duplicate handling need separate validation; these controls
instead compare the complete retained representation directly.

The fixed fixture uses the native planar source frame from D5S: descriptor
anchor `(37536,20928)`, source origin `(-200,-200)`, clip center `(300,300,0)` and
half extent `(700,700,0)`. Native requests are local Int2 coordinates, not these
source-origin coordinates or saved beacon position. Complete source-cell seed
keys, owner key, resource order, DNAM blocks, rarity weights, clip and native
settings are held fixed. Each repeat clears the collections before reconstruction;
cross-process comparison avoids dependence on retained engine holders.

Native per-cell `0x14079a330` and competition/shape routines execute in owned
private pages. Supplied allocators, coordinate conversion keys and collection
getters replace the surrounding engine. Native math thunk/import binding is
explicit in the method/receipts. No game is launched, and no generated retail
population is read. The independent RG1 planar oracle supplies every expected
point and bound, including competition; agreement is checked resource by
resource rather than inferred from plausible diagrams.

## Why equal ordinary masks are insufficient for a broader claim

Generator `0x14079b520` preserves original geographic query R8 at
`0x14079b527`. It restores that query for ordinary low-nibble sampling near
`0x14079b8dd` and again for the Special path near `0x14079bf06`; the latter
sampling at `0x14079c005` uses high bits at `0x14079c009`. The native ordinary
stage begins after query sampling. Thus two requests with the same ordinary
mask have equal fixture inputs only when all other effective stage inputs also
match. The fixture does not execute the entire generator.

For this fixed main-file source, the missing applicability premise is now
positively checked. Fresh installed-definition reconstruction in
`extra-inputs03.json` (earlier specialist `geometry-special-inputs02.json`)
resolves BIOM `0x00145440`, effective RSGD `0x00258050` and component slots
`[0x000057d0,0,0x000057d4,0,0,0]`: Special has no eligible candidate and the
BIOM-local Everywhere slot is zero. Native null-slot branches bypass both extra
passes. The original geographic query's second use therefore does not contribute
an omitted Special output in this fixed contract. This is conditional main-file
initialization, not a read of B2's effective loaded component.

Final controls label distinct supplied original queries `[4,1]` and `[5,0]`.
The pinned atlas in the fixed descriptor maps both to byte `0x03`; their complete
native populations agree. Query `[4,-1]` maps to byte `0x00` and differs. The
query-to-atlas step is independently computed, and native per-cell calls receive
the resulting fixed effective inputs. The compiled input-factorization proof
from D5T and the positive extra-pass elimination compose a call-level full
fixed-source population equivalence. This is stronger than mere mask agreement,
and remains distinct from executing the whole generator or verifying retail
inputs. In particular, it does not fill B2's unrecorded losing-query sample.

For independent construction equivalence, also bind source allocation/content,
descriptor and effective component, loaded/remapped definitions and resource
order, raster values and readiness, generation settings, source-cell seed keys,
Special applicability/value, clipping and relevant owner/world state. Any missing
material input makes equivalence unproved. Different ordinary masks are not
automatically unequal for every source either; their effective weights may
coincide or a particular stage may emit nothing. Here the complete native
population comparison establishes an actual difference for the fixed source.

Within-session holder retention skips regeneration and preserves previously
constructed geometry. It is a separate mechanism from the cold fixed-input
repetitions above. B2's sample `0x3`, generator return and visited token store
do not disclose its complete population. Neither B2 nor player-reported stable
extractor locations is a controlled full-geometry cold comparison.
