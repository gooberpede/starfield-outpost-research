## The requested 20% scepticism scenario

A hypothetical per-construction contrary-mask probability of 20% would give
`0.8^5 = 0.32768` for five mask-3 outcomes **if** five independent, identically
distributed trials had been observed. That is a sensitivity calculation, not
the likelihood model of this history. These are five constructions in four
sessions; A0/A1 share a process and a controlled departure/return. Inputs,
ingresses and instrumentation versions differ, and there is no justified
independence or stationary success probability. Even grouping four sessions
as independent would itself be an additional unsupported premise.

For an illustrative 50% contrary probability the same unsupported IID arithmetic
would yield `0.5^5 = 0.03125`; for 0% it yields 1. These examples demonstrate
sensitivity to a chosen model. They neither estimate the actual probability nor
set an acceptance threshold. The specified 20% concern remains a reason to
investigate readiness/service mechanisms, not proof of impossibility or a
pass/fail probability. No binomial confidence interval is fitted to these data.

Without a measure over the permitted schedules, a feasible contrary path can
have unknown, very small or substantial prevalence. Candidate counts alone
provide no useful numerical lower/upper prevalence bound beyond `[0,1]`; even
that interval describes ignorance rather than a fitted forecast. A supported
structural exclusion would instead need a proof over ordinary paths, while a
complete feasible counterexample establishes possibility without frequency.

The smallest future measurement, if prevalence is pursued, must retain a
predeclared ordinary route, qualified cold/reconstruction denominator, actual
effective inputs, contrary outcomes and instrumentation limits. It should test
frontier readiness/service versus later overtake through existing task/provider/
owner boundaries before accumulating an arbitrary number of masks. The
[report](d5aq-report.md) states what offline reachability establishes. No live
measurement, new observer or prospective prediction is authorized here.
