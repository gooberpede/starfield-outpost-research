## Connected lifecycle model

Geographic source publication supplies raw source aliases to ordinary work.
The selected source's content-derived token is checked under ProcGen shared
protection. A token hit bypasses before the pointer-keyed map; it does not fetch
a holder. Unvisited contenders leave the reader before acquiring exclusive
map protection. One miss can insert and construct while a previously checked
contender later hits the same holder. Density aggregate reuse can bypass this
provider sequence earlier still.

The holder owns resource collections whose copied point arrays survive their
generation temporaries. Point readers and area/Workshop consumers use resident
geometry with their own ownership and refresh contracts. A returned resource
identity/list is not a retained source or contour lease. A Grid reference also
does not protect a raw selected source from retirement. Actual spanning manager
readers, including qualified nested terrain work, supply conditional protection
against another thread's exclusive movement; the reader must really span use.

Normal shifts preserve overlapping sources and can retire nonoverlapping ones.
Retirement and Grid clear erase relevant pointer-map owners without clearing
visited membership. Final contour destruction depends on actual reference
counts; fresh native controls show a second genuine owner preserving a
collection after one map is cleared. They do not establish a long-lived
Workshop contour owner. Broad reset clears map and visited storage. Under
the ordinary full-load graph, complementary revert/loader predicates clear
ProcGen even when the saved-context comparison is compatible.

Type-3 context/descriptors/components and type-7 PCM seed/content/placements
restore separate inputs. Their concrete readers and preparation consumers do
not deserialize the old ProcGen region arrays. A later demand can construct
from restored/rebuilt inputs, produce no output under failed prerequisites,
reuse a surviving holder, or bypass before construction according to the
qualified state. No demand means reset alone produces no geometry.

The established extractor is a downstream consumer with a different lifecycle.
Placement intersects current point-admitted resources with effective produced
resource definitions. Registration and production use effective resource
properties and resource-keyed producer rows, with object/Workshop restoration
and current operating checks. They are not a contour identifier. An extractor
continuing to produce therefore cannot prove unchanged deposits; an area-list
match, placement success and inventory increase are different observations.
