## Why these are new outpost maps

The original 38 D5U figures remain byte-identical source-level evidence.
Normalizing each source anchor to zero also normalized its generation context;
that changed packed seed operands in some cases. Translating those finished
paths cannot recover the historical-frame result. The
[two-frame diagnostic](d5u/outposts/coordinate-join.svg) compares U01's frozen
30-path historical-frame model with its 28-path prospective native execution.
The same historical disk can be located on both, but on the prospective panel
it is expressly a reference, not a historical prediction.

C1 therefore reuses the authenticated D5 frozen populations and D5A conditional
source outputs under their original historical descriptors. It performs no
procedural regeneration or new native execution. D5A's earlier broad mask
neighborhoods are restricted to D5U's qualified finite support: U13–U15 use 8;
U17/U18 and U22/U24 use 0; only U16, U19–U21, U23 and U25 retain two alternatives.
Separately feasible alternatives are separate source panels. No Cartesian product
is called jointly achievable, and no particular historical winner is selected.

The frozen cohort's failed source branches are not omitted. Their 13 qualified
source populations come from D5A. Independent finite-halo re-enumeration also
found nine sources outside the D5U comparative census. These retain separate
C1-X01–C1-X09 identities. They are essential to avoid an empty or incomplete
VII-b map and to represent VIII-b's upper source row correctly.
