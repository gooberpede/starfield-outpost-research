## Accepted and rejected explanations

| Explanation | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Same live allocation and owned complete content can survive ordinary movement | Supported under the bounded movement/overlap predicates with continuity across any other events. An actual hit or map traversal establishes reuse. Mere residence or lack of demand establishes no use. |
| Equal deposits or resource list prove retention | Rejected. D5AK independently compares complete equal bytes after actual old-content ending and new generation. Workshop lists can also remain cached separately. |
| Visited means map hit, or holder erase means immediate free and fresh sampling | Rejected. D5AC distinguishes both decisions. D5AH control receipts preserve a referenced collection after removal and emulate an equal-token bypass after retirement. Those controls do not establish a retail missing-deposit bug. |
| A Grid clear, context match or observer epoch selects the same native outcome every time | Rejected. D5AJ qualifies source-disjoint clears without a true reset. Same owner/component installation can take false reset and clear both stores. Full reload takes one of the complementary branches under the ordinary stable-setting contract. |
| Different native sample opportunities imply changed complete geometry | Rejected by D5AK. D5AQ native controls establish a mechanism for variation under fixed remaining inputs. D5AS independently supplies complete differing retail geometry without direct per-snapshot causal classification. |
| Restored PCM or producer resource identity is restored source geometry | Rejected in the audited channel. The writer/reader pairs and separate clear show distinct state owners. |

## Smallest optional discriminator

One same-area out-and-back from the already-qualified Feynman V source, with
a complete pre-hop baseline and post-return comparison, would answer the
highest-value player distinction for that itinerary. The missing edge is
the actual target owner's retention, retirement or broad reset and the
first subsequent qualified decision. The recorded B2 contention, D5AJ
other-Grid clears, D5AK exterior trip and static receiver branches cannot
identify the state of a different, unrecorded warm trip. Save bytes and
another geometry-only snapshot cannot recover that history either.

The minimum evidence is the reported marker action bound to the process,
owner/component and owning Grid events, source births/retirements, exact target
erase or reset with visited state, and target hit, bypass or miss/sample with
complete before/after geometry in the same source/frame. No extra forced
refresh is needed. If there is no qualified post-return demand, record that
bounded outcome separately from a measured bypass. This single contrast
requires separately authorized live acquisition with the applicable protection
and integrity gates. It answers that itinerary rather than assigning a rule
to every hop. No new trial is indispensable to the conditional D5AV answers.
