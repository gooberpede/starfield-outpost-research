## Binary, transport and arming contract

The raw sidecar is `sor-d5ai-raw/2`. Its little-endian header is 456 bytes:
`<22Q24sII9Q44sI16Q`. The u32 before the two 64-byte lifetime markers is
`preunlock_seen`; the corresponding earlier tail
qword is the real pre-release `scan_result`. Complete scan qualification requires
both witnesses, result 1, iterator equal to capacity, matching copied capacity,
and unchanged epoch/hazard/loss conditions. The ordinary cache-skip path does
not execute acquisition and supplies no absence certificate.
The reader also requires `sor-d5ai-context/1` with both whole-context sites.
Earlier development captures lacking that capability are preserved but refused
by the final readiness consumer, rather than silently promoted.

Each occupied source-map entry retains its source-key pointer and holder
pointer separately. Resource entries retain FormID, collection identity and the
region vector count/capacity/data. Regions also retain point capacity/data and
their original region address. Empty/null and count-zero allocated-spare
backings remain distinct diagnostics; semantic comparison uses represented
content. No retired address is read again.
Regions retain all 24 raw header bytes and every ordered 16-byte point.
Resource hash-slot order is represented order, not a claimed generation order.
The independent normalizer may sort unique FormIDs under its explicit native
justification, while preserving region and point order. Region header padding
is retained raw but excluded from semantic comparison only where native copying
proves it uninitialized.

Capture states are OK, READ_FAULT, MALFORMED, ARENA_FULL, INTERRUPTED,
EXCLUSIVE_RECURSION, CONSUMER_UNFINISHED and CONSUMER_PARTIAL. Interruptions take
precedence over a complete-population claim. Invalid snapshots preserve their
available bytes and diagnostics; they never become an empty valid population.
The complete resident representation is distinct from successful intended native
generation: `native_quality` remains UNKNOWN unless separately demonstrated.

Four preallocated slots each contain a 2,097,152-byte payload arena. This is a
memory-integrity bound, not a total scientific event/time/evidence quota.
Arena exhaustion is explicit; no-slot loss is sticky and fails recorder health.
The [actual worker extension](../../../runtime/construction-winner/d5ai_transport.inc)
writes an owned snapshot, flushes it, then publishes an identity/hash/request-bound
receipt. It retains write/flush/receipt failures and incomplete process exits.
Both caller-available drive floors remain 40,000,000,000 bytes.

The [geometry controller](../../../runtime/construction-winner/d5ai_controller.py)
uses the existing exclusive P4 process/worker/session controller. Publishing the
arm file is PUBLICATION_PENDING. A fresh challenge created after publication
must acknowledge this exact request in `geometry_armed` before GEOMETRY_ARMED.
A delayed worker, stale healthy ACK or reconnect cannot substitute for that
acknowledgment. If a natural scan consumes the arm first, the controller returns
CAPTURED_BEFORE_ARM_ACK; inspect that capture without requesting an extra query.
Small live receipt checks remain byte-unverified; full raw authentication belongs
to offline postflight. No CLI command supplies the separately required live GO.
Geometry, context and lifetime plan rows bind exact site order, reason, byte length,
module-relative address and guard hash. Duplicate new JSON keys are rejected;
the new geometry schema marker occurs once in worker JSON.

The [raw reader](../../../runtime/construction-winner/d5ai_read_geometry.py)
checks identity-bound flushed bytes, exact header and nested census, payload
exhaustion, request/snapshot identity and actual completion predicates. Missing
receipt, truncated bytes or nonzero capture status cannot establish availability.
It does not manufacture source lifetime or frame truth from its own JSON output.


## Baseline content-ending journal

Independent review found that map absence alone could not distinguish an
externally retained baseline population from final content teardown. The
[ending proof](d5ai-population-ending-proof.md) supplies the source-rooted typed
coverage. The separately versioned [journal](../../../runtime/construction-winner/d5ai_lifetime.h)
observes complete functions at `0x1407aab10`, `0x1407ac260` and `0x1407ac2f0`.
Their original five-byte RBX shadow stores are replayed by leaf trampolines;
32-byte body preimages and full relay-page hashes remain guarded. The receivers
are holder+0x10, collection+0x08 and collection+0x08 respectively. The first two
mean `HOLDER_RESOURCE_MAP_ENDING` and `COLLECTION_GEOMETRY_ENDING`. They do not mean
the outer allocation has been freed. The third is a defensive collection
mutation invalidator. BEGIN permanently invalidates the old content identity;
END records original-body exit, including an explicit exceptional status 16.
BEGIN status must be zero; END status is zero or 16.

The independent original-native specialist controls establish final-reference
and external-retention behavior. The candidate host cases exercise actual hooks,
queue, worker, frames and acknowledgments over explicitly supplied owned engine
policy; they do not claim an occurrence in the game. Baseline collection IDs and
all allocated region/point backing diagnostics are copied before retirement.
Later conclusions use that copy and complete ending history without following
old pointers. Empty content with no allocated backing is reported separately
from positive allocated-spare backing survival.

`lifetimes.bin` starts with `<8Q32s>`, 96 bytes: magic, version 1, header/event sizes,
armed QPC/thread, epoch, reserved 0 and raw session identity SHA256. The arming point
occurs only after all guarded hooks are installed and before capture admission.
Each 96-byte event `<12Q>` records sequence, kind, phase, invocation, parent, thread, QPC,
receiver, allocation, status, epoch, hazards. A 16-byte frame `<4I>` records magic, size, low
sequence, CRC32. The separate 32,768-cell MPSC journal has fixed owned storage and
bounded producer retries; loss and integer overflow are sticky failures.

The [worker](../../../runtime/construction-winner/d5ai_lifetime_transport.inc)
flushes bytes before its identity/CRC/prefix-bound `lifetimes-flush.jsonl` ledger.
An authenticated core ACK or terminal binds the exact journal prefix and
watermark. A marker includes cursor, QPC, generation before/after, active before/after,
loss and failure. Only equal generations with zero active producers/loss/failure
and cursor within the durable prefix can advance coverage. Snapshot markers
bracket copying and completed consumption. Pausing a producer before record
publication cannot produce quiescent coverage. A positive onset predating the
baseline is required; an empty late-start journal proves no prior survival.

Admitted hooks always publish their balanced normal/exceptional closeout through
STOP. A disabled hook samples a sticky cutoff before forwarding an unlogged
original call. No later worker marker at or after that cutoff advances coverage;
prior durable prefixes remain usable. Controllers refuse further arming after a
cutoff or journal failure, while owned STOP remains available. The actual
admitted-STOP control checks worker waiting and balanced publication; a separate
disabled-gap control refuses the later snapshot despite a coherent raw copy.

matching source to an independent caller. It passes 11,123 assertions covering

is not a retail teardown latency or total engine-work estimate. The exact

The [episode adapter](../../../research/resource-retained-population/d5ai_episode.py)
