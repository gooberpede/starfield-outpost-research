## Uniqueness, unknowns and negative conclusions

The resident map is keyed by source pointer, not geographic key. Native
constructor deduplication is scoped to one Grid; it is not a universal unique-
geography constraint across all possible Grids or owners. One known target plus
an unidentified occupied holder therefore cannot establish unique target
selection without an independent exclusion for that holder. A weaker positive
claim could report one identified instance, but the comparison envelope needs
the unique target. This version retains the stronger guard. Absence likewise
requires complete identification or independently justified exclusion of every
occupied alternate; unknowns make absence INCONCLUSIVE.

Actual A0 has no unknown holder: 36 unique keys, one target key, 36 complete
birth/publication and generation chains, and nine source-disjoint Grid pairs
for each source interval. Synthetic duplicate-target and unknown-holder controls
refuse. Equal visited tokens cannot relabel geography, same geography under
another ProcGen cannot certify this output, and same-address new birth cannot
borrow an earlier generation certificate. Positive presence is supported here;
no target-absence or UNAVAILABLE claim is made.
