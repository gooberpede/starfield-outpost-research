## The finite coverage proof a reviewer must assess

A positive full ordering interpretation requires an authenticated ordinary-family
path census for this pinned build, admission before the relevant work/owner
lifetime, and observed completion of every material participant in the claimed
interval. In particular, every target provider used in the comparison needs its
own task allocation and source/invocation join; every relevant task needs enough
registration/readiness/claim snapshots to evaluate the claimed ordering. Unknown
earlier work is not excluded because the target callback was observed later.

The owner-lock history needs one supported watched owner, coherent generations,
known initial interval, no unknown holders/waiters, and matching operations through
the decision boundary. The generic gates do not depend on provider TLS. Final
bridges snapshot registry identity before the displaced operation and check the
receiver after that operation. Consequently, a watch published while an operation
is in flight produces an old-snapshot uncertain event instead of disappearing
through an earlier unmatched gate. If publication follows the post-operation
check, that operation already preceded the initial state sample. This closes the
specific crossing-gate race; it does not reconstruct pre-observation history or
make a retired pointer a stable identity.

Shared acquisition is captured in the generic helper, while shared decrement is
inline. The [fresh ordinary caller census](d5am-owner-lock-census.md) found three
additional shared decrements and two inline writer decrements beyond the earlier
proposal. The corrected sixteen-site instrument covers those demonstrated
ordinary receiver paths. These are typed release patches, not a universal
generic release hook. A reader acquired through the generic helper and released
elsewhere must leave the claimed cohort unresolved; absence of its release is
not evidence that it remained held. Independent review must assess the actual
receiver-rooted census and new-site qualification. Arbitrary computed replacement
clients remain outside that scoped source proof; byte guards or balanced fixture
outputs alone do not establish their absence.

Native bridge admission spans the displaced operation and capture, so a producer
paused after its atomic but before C++ admission remains included in the owned
STOP active handshake. Counter overflow/contention, sidecar loss, guard failure,
unflushed bytes, malformed records and an unfinished active producer are integrity
failures. Multiple legitimate owners, unknown baseline or lifetime uncertainty
are coverage refusals; healthy base recording can continue. Sidecar byte
completeness and scientific coverage are separate verdicts.

An independent reviewer must either establish these finite predicates from the
source census and actual trial record, or list the failed predicates and leave
the corresponding interpretation UNKNOWN. The readers deliberately do not
self-certify universal task or lock coverage. No finite offline fixture supplies
missing retail predicates.

## Discriminators and limits

The decisive alternatives do not require knowing why the OS ran one thread first.
They require sufficient observed participants and nonoverlapping native bounds.
For example, a pending callback is after that writer's XADD, whereas an exclusive
approach event is before that helper call. Thus `A.Pending < B.Approach <
B.Acquired` proves A was pending before B entered and B nevertheless acquired
first. Conversely, `B.Acquired < A.Approach` establishes B's acquisition before
A's helper entry. Intersecting bounds remain UNKNOWN. Event sequence and QPC
callback timestamps are not a universal order of atomic instants.

Explaining the upstream origin of every task or the OS scheduling decision is
unnecessary for those conditional discriminators. Explaining an earliest-eligible
or earliest-claimed winner is stronger: it requires the complete relevant work
set and adequate readiness/claim bounds. Missing registration, task lifetime,
provider join, owner participant, readiness transition or claim boundary is an
indispensable gap for that stronger claim. Unobserved OS motives do not excuse
one of those missing native edges.

The proposed finite run can still establish its actual request/mask/population
while an ordering explanation remains inconclusive. It cannot turn the historical
four different requests into a usual-winner distribution, exclude inconvenient
mask outcomes, add an unauthorized trial, or revise a preregistered prediction
after observation. Instrumentation, including native state preservation on
unrelated lock operations and the extra atomic admission counters, perturbs
timing. The result concerns the declared instrumented trial.
