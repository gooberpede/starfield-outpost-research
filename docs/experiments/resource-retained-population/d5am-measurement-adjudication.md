# D5AM prospective measurement adjudication

**NOT AUTHORIZED.** Independent disposition is recorded in
[the review record](d5am-review.md). This procedure
connects a future recording to a scientific claim. Byte-accounting PASS is a
necessary input, not a completeness certificate. No D5AM retail trial exists.
The [native observation predicates](d5am-observation-adjudication.md) supply
the detailed stage and owner-field census obligations for this procedure.

## Separate decisions

1. Authenticate the exact reviewed plan, producer, installed site preimages,
   interpreter, controller, readers and prediction. Require fresh episode-bound
   authority and protected preflight. The historical AC01 cap breach remains
   FAIL and requires its own explicit maintainer disposition before any future
   live approval. Neither this procedure nor an AI review provides permission.
2. After owned STOP and normal exit, require fresh protected postflight and
   complete core, geometry, lifetime, causal and scheduler accounting. Preserve
   every partial result. Actual loss, corruption, missing required fields or
   identity/flush/terminal mismatch refuses the affected scientific inference.
3. Qualify the particular target source and construction using positive
   birth/publication, owning Grid, allocation/lifetime and frame witnesses.
   Apply the existing D5AJ source-scoped invalidation method. An unrelated
   owning-Grid clear or global epoch increment cannot invalidate the target
   merely by changing an observer counter. Unknown relevant endings, reuse or
   history gaps cannot be repaired by equal pointers or equal geometry.
4. Identify the actual protected target miss, generating invocation, direct
   sample and successful token store. Report its original signed request and
   translated descriptor separately. Search the complete qualified process
   history for earlier target constructions; a new process alone proves
   neither effective cold state nor earliest eligibility.
5. Adjudicate target/task/owner coverage explicitly before interpreting why
   that invocation won. Keep an evidence reference for every accepted fact and
   every remaining unknown. A locally clean association is useful even if a
   different allocation later becomes uncertain; an affected interval itself
   remains unqualified.

The exact native/site audit must establish whether the implemented boundaries
cover each mandatory claim above. These rules do not assert that every possible
upstream engine action or operating-system scheduling decision is measured.
They prohibit reporting a complete causal explanation where an indispensable
discriminator is still missing. The final independent review must challenge
that distinction against Q2 and Q3 rather than accepting table completion.

## Prediction disposition and series accounting

The immutable version-1 forecast applies to the first eligible new trial only.
An independently reviewed adjudication supplies six explicit qualification
facts to `d5am_prediction_oracle.py`, with references to the actual evidence and
review. This small rule evaluator does not authenticate those facts. Required
causal coverage must be substantiated by the preceding audit, not set true
because a parser emits JOINED or the final population looks plausible.
Here required causal coverage means the approved mandatory stages, identities
and relevant participant scope are intact. It does not require disjoint timing
bounds or a decisive explanation of OS progress. A genuinely measured overlap
can leave ordering UNKNOWN while the mask forecast is evaluable. Missing
mandatory events, relevant unqualified cohorts or unknown source/task history
remain actual coverage gaps. Apply this distinction before inspecting the mask.

For an eligible constructor/sample, mask 3 confirms this one forecast and every
other nibble 0 through 15 contradicts it. Invalid-domain observations are
preserved as anomalies without masking or replacement. A missing qualification
leaves this forecast INCONCLUSIVE while retaining any narrower direct sample.
Equal geometry, nonempty output and a particular request are never filters.
No trial means NOT TESTED.

Record all approved attempts, their eligible-construction denominator, original
requests/masks, causal witness classes and every refusal or inconclusive result.
After each episode, ask which unresolved mechanism another separately approved
ordinary trial could discriminate. Recurrence within this pinned route/context
is descriptive evidence; a causal rule needs contrasting progress conditions.
No fixed trial quota, automatic continuation or probability estimate follows.
