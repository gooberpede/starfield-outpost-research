## Checkpoint 4 — Independent REWORK and native boundary closure

The engineering review found two concrete reducer defects: prior map hit followed
by a later miss could receive false first attribution, and the header's low
32-bit sequence was compared with an unbounded count. Both are corrected and
preserved as adversarial/boundary controls. Terminal success now differs from
admitted-record finalization and native coverage; damage cannot be called a
complete recording. Exact concurrent request/mask and invocation/thread identities
are checked. Volatile GPR, XMM0–5, flags and thread LastError sentinels exercise

The native completeness challenge found a real consumed-descriptor gap. Mutable
manager pre-copy did not prove engine consumption. [The architecture correction](d5x-architecture.md#consumed-descriptor-correction-before-final-qualification)
adds the filtered normalizer CALL witness, version 0.2.0/schema 2. Its controlled
fixture changes and restores manager storage while retaining the different
engine-local copy. Descriptor padding remains raw/nonsemantic. The inspected
source shift writer changes local anchors, not the copied geographic key fields.
All previous schema-1 packages and readers remain untouched in development scratch.

The practical cold-anchor challenge followed ordinary module registration/reset
order and late terrain MultiFiles retention. Landscape is registered/reset before
ProcGen in the inspected forward loops. Late MultiFiles cleanup does not release
its raw ordinary/overlay Grid pointers. Fresh pinned disassembly and reproducible
methods are retained as `cold-order02.*`; no required post-anchor clear was found
on this route. Actual later invalidation still rejects an episode.

Lookup now records the actual native owner, source key operand and decision
epoch independently of the provider's entry epoch. An old provider spanning reset
cannot hide earlier uncertain activity under its old epoch and make a new source
allocation look first. Unscoped native activity is a coverage uncertainty, not
proof of absence. Acquisition continues; first-attribution needs a new clean
cold certificate where required.

The scientific reviewer returned REWORK for an untested material avenue:
deterministic native reset/provider interleavings. New event-controlled cases
hold old providers before lookup, force both old-first and fresh-first lookup
orders across reset, and admit work after native unlock but before reset-wrapper
closeout. The build22 subset executes those actual hooks and independently reduces
to zero, one and zero qualified chains respectively. The fresh-first result is
the fresh `(10,11)` / Mask 3 query, never the delayed old `(-2,-2)` / Mask 0.
Concurrent multi-source calls now differ from same-source competing calls; eight
providers are held alive before any lookup so concurrency is demonstrated rather
than inferred from thread creation. They reduce to nine and two chains including
the earlier baseline episode. Final full qualification remains in progress.

The final DLL has no embedded native emitter excerpts. Those are a separately
hashed LOCAL-ONLY fixture input in the disposable package, excluded from the
