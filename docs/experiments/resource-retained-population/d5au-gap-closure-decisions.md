## Extractor continuity and optional observation

D5AH distinguishes new placement's current point admission from established
resource-keyed registration and production. The writer saves the resource
FormID and producer identity/bookkeeping. The loader resolves the resource
and producer, while the ordinary due update uses the row's map key and
rechecks operating conditions without automatically resampling the point.
Source retirement does not itself rewrite that key. A correctly restored or
registered ordinary extractor can retain its association and produce when
its handle and Workshop resolve and its effective properties, power, capacity,
recipe, current eligibility and output destination permit it. Failed
resolution or registration, a halted or unpowered producer, or a full
receiver can prevent output. Saved association and actual production require
separate evidence. [D5AH continuity](d5ah-extractor-continuity.md) ·
[Lifecycle matrix](d5ah-lifecycle-matrix.md) · [Saved owner join](d5ah-extractor-owner.md).

The maintainer accepts this mechanism as sufficient for the present objective.
The original empirical GC-34 requirement remains untested and optional.
If an application needs that validation, use a separately scoped comparison
of one matched extractor/Workshop across a verified changed-geometry
transition. The comparison would bind complete resident geometry, the verified
lifetime transition, and the producer row on each side, then measure actual
output at the selected destination and record operating gates and point
eligibility independently. D5AK has equal rebuilt geometry and D5AS has
different source geometry across starts, with no matched extractor/output
witness in either case.
[D5AH prospective comparison](d5ah-experiment-design.md).
