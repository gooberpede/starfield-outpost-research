## Competing explanations

The hypothesis that geographic order fixes the winner fails at two independent
boundaries. The native dispatcher can publish and rotate callbacks while other
workers claim them; even an earlier provider/check can reach the source-map
decision later, as B2 shows. A supposed mask-3 preference from early registration
has only a head start measured in registration steps, without a proven minimum
amount of worker service. It is not a measured or guaranteed preference.

The alternative that unfinished startup definitions explain B2 is unsupported.
Ordinary startup admission waits for its initialization phase; per-request
readiness and deferred raster loading have their own scopes. B2 has no recorded
loader completion clock or worker caller chain. Those missing historical fields
cannot be filled with Lodge residency, a scheduler model or pointer equality.

Authority also differs from successful geometry. Metadata/map acquisition can
fail inside the generator; the provider still attempts visitation after a normal
return. A failed visited reserve leaves a holder that another already-admitted
request can reuse. Grid retirement and broad reset have different effects.
The [method ledger](d5ae-methods-and-tests.md) preserves these adverse cases.
