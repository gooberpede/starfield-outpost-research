## Q1 — What ordinary execution selects or favours

Initialization builds the source index and installs context. Valid focus enables
asynchronous region prewarming; ordinary cell preparation and admitted bounded
LOD work provide separate coordinate demands. Density work can become ready
while registrations continue. Layer work has additional preparation/dependency
conditions and prepares its density neighbors before its own provider call.
The [normal-path model](d5w-normal-path-model.md) gives original coordinates,
locks, service pumps, callback bindings, warm/cold assumptions and both first
constructing misses, including the ordinary terrain and grouped-cell branches.

Under D5S's common normalized arrival, all 196 support queries lie in a
1,720-density-key expansion. Owner/back and peer/front continuations, including
native import rotation and source-selection barriers, reach `(10,11)` / mask 3
and `(-2,-2)` / mask 0 respectively. Neither continuation requires an exception,
failed allocation, altered register or patched retail process. Their deliberately
delayed competing work is a scheduler premise, not a measured common occurrence.

An independent early completeness challenge required incremental activation
rather than reusing only those selected late-batch witnesses. The first target
density registration is `(-2,11)` at index 419; the first mask-0 target density
registration is `(-2,-1)` at index 833. Resident prerequisites therefore allow
early mask-3 work before that later mask-0 work exists. The 414 index steps are
an admission opportunity, not a time measurement. Layer `(-2,11)` independently
refutes a density-only exclusion argument: none of its four density neighbors
can emit the target, so its preparation dependency must be retained.

The new native queue controls change claims after the same registrations are
activated in different batches. Immediate draining claims `(-2,11)` for both
owner and peer. A whole batch reproduces the two D5S claims. Batch 875 yields
owner `(-2,-2)` and peer `(-2,10)`. There is therefore no owner-mask identity or
global FIFO rule. These controlled claims do not execute the complete engine's
nodes and do not establish an empirical preference for Mask 3.

The operational distinction is: locality, readiness and queue-side rules are
real policies; a source-specific canonical winner is not established. A short
prerequisite chain can have a head start when its inputs are resident and worker
service is available. Which route wins in retail also depends on other admitted
work, file readiness, service occupancy and source-map lock arrival. No numerical
probability or ordinary-versus-pathological frequency label is justified here.

## Q2 — What becomes authoritative

The first exclusive pointer-map miss serializes construction for that live
source entry. The original query survives into descriptor conversion and raster
sampling. Source anchor, generator origin, source BIOM, nominal footprint centre,
clipping centre and outpost centre have separate roles; none replaces that query
on the inspected construction path. [Sampling positions](d5w/sampling.svg) show
the distinct candidates with hypothetical authority visibly marked.

The resulting wrapper has incidental first-miss authority within its retained
episode. Cache hit/visited bypass prevents this path from selecting a better,
more central or later query. That demonstrates duplicate suppression and retained
stability, not proof of a performance motive or intended canonical mask. Visited
is appended without a successful-population test; an empty/partial holder is not
automatically retried into a preferred result.

These controls distinguish sampled rarity, effective weights, actual geometry
and consumer admission. Numeric low-nibble values are not resource-membership
bits. The source BIOM and resource order remain fixed inputs, not a biome lookup
at the winning query. Equal weights suffice only with the other native inputs
fixed; unequal weights alone do not prove distinct geometry.

Other supplied contributing layers cannot conceal this Ionic Liquids difference:
all 29 of their Ionic Liquids regions, including both U23 alternatives, lie
outside the native point predicate's XY broadphase bound at this point. Their
smallest outside-bound margin exceeds 179 native units. That last exclusion is
STATIC/DERIVED from exact bounds and the native predicate, rather than a newly
executed scan. It does not require asserting a particular joint source-winner
combination. The per-layer checks are retained in the point-comparison data.

Movement below the shift threshold does not itself reset the source map. Ordinary
shifts can preserve overlapping populations and retire sources outside coverage.
Retirement erases the pointer-map entry but does not clear visited. A subsequent
equal visited identity can therefore suppress construction; a new pointer alone
is not evidence of fresh generation. Broad manager/module reset clears both
stores. Taken load-phase reset, owner/component replacement or a new manager
can admit a new successful demand. A drain alone does not prove fresh population.

Fresh loading and regeneration are thus distinct from revisiting a retained
holder. Old process pointers do not persist as identities. No indispensable
persisted producer of source geometry was identified; the conditional save-read
trigger was not met. Existing authenticated authored save-derived centre/frame
tables and the C1 archived receipts suffice for the coordinate references. No
protected save contents were newly read or altered.

## Q4 — Competing explanations and evidence hierarchy

**EXPLICIT DESIGN EVIDENCE:** Bethesda Support's
[distribution guidance](https://help.bethesda.net/app/answers/detail/a_id/61060/~/how-are-planetary-resources-distributed%3F)
says different players may see different distributions. Its
[gathering guidance](https://help.bethesda.net/app/answers/detail/a_id/61082/~/resource-gathering---tips-%2526-tricks---starfield)
associates extractors with resource fields. Neither page specifies native source
identity, query choice, cache authority, a scheduler invariant or reload stability
for this executable. They do not discriminate the competing native mechanisms.
This is read-only consultation, credited as SOR-S-0153, not implementation reuse.

**INFERRED OPERATIONAL CONTRACT:** ordinary locality and dependency machinery
admit query-dependent first construction, serialize a source's first map miss
and conditionally preserve its copied geometry. Singleton controls show how
different queries can converge on one result. Distinct area/point consumers
explain why names, shapes and resource-point admission need different contracts.

**PERMITTED/EXECUTED BEHAVIOUR:** both D5S compiled first-miss continuations,
D5T/U native populations, D5W's batch-sensitive queue controls and the new fixed
point predicate have the stated finite evidence envelopes. They do not prove
intent, retail rates or that every integer support query wins.

**UNKNOWN:** a dominant retail arrival path, historical loaded winner, deliberate
acceptance of every interleaving, and any missing/swapped-resource bug cause.
No independently established canonical-result invariant is contradicted by these
alternatives, so this study does not diagnose a defect. The bounded source-lifetime
gap remains a separate D5S result and is not used as a bug explanation.

The [hypothesis matrix](d5w-hypotheses.csv) preserves falsifiers and counterexamples.
A strict canonical mask under the normalized arrival is contradicted by the
paired witnesses. A typical but unenforced higher-level preference remains
possible; the timing premises needed to demonstrate it are stated explicitly.
The supported architecture combines incidental first-miss authority and retained
stability, without upgrading that result to proof of developer intention.
