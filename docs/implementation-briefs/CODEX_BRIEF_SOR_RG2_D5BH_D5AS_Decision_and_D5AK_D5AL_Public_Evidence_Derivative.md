## 6. Acceptance criteria

**AC01 — Reading and authority:** Complete relevant required reading and applied-reading record, independently verified; initial/final repo and worktree state; authorized read/write paths; one active sub-agent maximum; no unauthorized operation.

**AC02 — D5AS resolution fidelity:** The maintainer's decision is durably recorded with no change to scientific confidence or original acquisition qualification, no invented winner/mask/IID claim, no new acquisition archive and no false hosted/cleared state; D5AS-BODY remains conditioned on exact staged/hosted verification.

**AC03 — Source authenticity:** Actual original D5AK/D5AL materials exist and are positively hash/source/procedure verified; unavailable originals or missing decisive records are expressly reported rather than simulated from prose.

**AC04 — Feasibility gate:** A reviewed claim-by-claim indispensability analysis proves the proposed bounded subset preserves the core identity, lifetimes, chronology, owner/source/frame and negative evidence; otherwise the task returns a justified `CONDITIONAL/INFEASIBLE` stop without a misleading candidate.

**AC05 — Exact geometry:** The three full 5,090-byte original normalized bodies are independently reconstructed and compared byte by byte under their correct schema, with source and actual frame pins, original A0 identity and nontrivial negative controls.

**AC06 — Lifecycle and process proof:** Source/holder and three content lifetime endings, replacement construction and separate process construction are checked using actual events and sufficiently complete ordering/ownership context. The verifier rejects plausible same-hash but wrong-identity, no-end or alternate-source cases.

**AC07 — Frozen prediction:** The unchanged historical PRED-01 v1 input/oracle is exactly identified and the original A0/B0 result is independently testable, preserving all limitations and distinction from retrospective prediction.

**AC08 — Public reader isolation:** A clean-checkout reader verifies the candidate without `.local-work/`, game installation or private paths to the claimed bounded tier. Any lawful original-game prerequisite is declared; the verifier never claims broader evidence than it has.

**AC09 — Independent review:** Separate technical/evidence and scientific sufficiency challenges cover exact final candidate bytes, original-source reconciliation, completeness/omission, negative tests, disclosure/privacy and interpretation. Final review returns explicit `ACCEPT/REWORK/BLOCKED` by criterion; a synthetic-only test is not a source audit.

**AC10 — Publication boundaries:** Candidate artifact, hashes and transfer requirements are recorded; raw source and original archives unchanged; package itself uncommitted/unpublished; rights and disclosure remain subject to final approval; release B0–B6 and run-card gates remain pending.

**AC11 — Documentation and validators:** All changed claims/evidence/action references and counts join; original reports/reviews and accepted confidence remain intact; appropriate verifiers and negative controls pass, with any tests not run disclosed.

**AC12 — Delivery:** Provide an exact changed/new file inventory, original-source and candidate hashes/sizes, phase outcomes and gaps, independent-review result, remaining maintainer decisions, and a **proposed conventional commit message**. **Do not stage, commit or push.**
