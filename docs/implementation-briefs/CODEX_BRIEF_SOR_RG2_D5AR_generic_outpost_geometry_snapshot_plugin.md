## 1. Purpose, scientific motivation and agreed decisions

Produce a **reusable, minimal SFSE plugin** that can capture **existing, complete resource geometry at the player's current outpost on demand**, writing one immutable, timestamped snapshot file per command invocation, with a separately qualified offline decoder/comparator. It must not be hardcoded to Feynman V, a particular resource, a single population holder or one source; use an explicit, defensible definition of the captured area and source set. The immediate planned use is a **full flight of 30 qualified, fresh-process cold-start observations at Feynman V Cu IL F**, retained and analysed together *after* collection. Do **not** stop the planned series at the first mask-0 result.

The attraction of the approach is avoiding the continuous scheduling, lock and construction hooks of observer 0.9.3. The snapshot command executes **after** ordinary generation, so the plugin should remain quiescent during the construction race. A post-construction snapshot can prove **represented geometry**; it cannot, without an independently valid inference, prove the **historical constructing request or sampled mask**. Preserve that distinction.

Existing bounded facts to reuse, not reinterpret:

- D5AQ-C1 fixed-input, Feynman V source `0:37536:20928:0029d20e`, owner/body `0x0005e0de`: 196 request coordinates; 28 conditionally sample mask `0x0`, 168 mask `0x3`. **This is not a winner-probability distribution.**
- Fixed-input complete geometry: mask `0x0` = three resources, **74 regions / 193 points**; mask `0x3` = three resources, **78 regions / 180 points**. Equality must be checked using full independently verified normalized geometry, not counts or inferred mask alone.
- Qualified retail A0, A1 and B0 (plus N2) provide authenticated mask-3 geometry and provenance. The target mask-3 complete normalized representation in the D5AK/D5AL baseline is 5,090 bytes with SHA-256 `0c05d4a4c458b533d1ec7ae3acc8b34698ac48b91ff2965224e5c37ca055da96`. Do not substitute other source, frame or historical session merely because geometry resembles it.
- D5AI established current ProcGen pointer-map, holder, collection, region/point, reader-lock and owner-lifetime semantics; D5AJ proved that global observer epoch changes and other-Grid clears are not blanket source invalidators. Neither established that an **arbitrary console callback** may safely traverse those structures or that a map census automatically identifies an outpost's deposits.
- N2/D5AP establish normal construction can use variable requests; the question now is **the distribution of actual resulting populations**, not a new attempt to trace all scheduler races.

## 2. Frozen controlling questions and success standard

**D5AR-Q1 — On-demand safe observation.** Can an SFSE-invoked, read-only command safely and completely copy currently resident resource geometry, without executing a generator, changing visited/map state, forcing a refresh, installing winner-critical instrumentation or traversing unprotected borrowed pointers? Establish the exact callback/lock/thread/owner-lifetime contract by original native instructions and executed owned controls.

**D5AR-Q2 — Generic outpost and source attribution.** Can the command identify the current outpost and distinguish the sources/populations actually relevant to its spatial context from unrelated resident sources, *without* hardcoding Feynman identifiers? Define the strongest supported relation and its limits, particularly multiple sources, overlapping areas, nonresident populations, missing/ambiguous owners and absent current-outpost identity. If a full outpost census cannot be supported, report and preserve a rigorously scoped capture rather than falsely calling it complete.

**D5AR-Q3 — Durable capture and valid comparison.** Can the plugin produce a self-describing, atomically finalized, unique snapshot and can an independent offline reader authenticate every represented resource/region/point, verify source/frame comparability, classify exact content equality, and compare Feynman outcomes to qualified references without treating counts or a guess as a sampled mask?

**D5AR-Q4 — Experimental readiness, not experimental completion.** Is the exact plugin, command, format, safety/STOP procedure, single-live-pilot proposal and **separate 30-qualified-sample protocol** independently qualified, including a prespecified aggregate-analysis method and no outcome-dependent early stopping? A passing offline fixture is **not** a retail pilot or evidence of normal mask frequency.

All four questions are frozen at this scope. Incomplete mandatory proof is **INCOMPLETE/REWORK** while authorized leads remain; a genuine unresolvable dependency may be **BLOCKED/ESCALATE** with full barrier evidence. An independently accepted narrower feasibility result does not silently close Q1–Q4 or GC-10.

## 3. Required research and design — follow actual native semantics

### 3.1 SFSE command delivery and startup footprint

- Examine the pinned 1.16.244.0 Starfield/SFSE environment and repository's licensing, headers, tooling and installed plugin registration options. **Prove** that the selected command-registration technique functions for this build, with an appropriate bounded offline stand-in. Do not guess a Bethesda console API or introduce a third-party command framework without reviewing its actual availability, provenance, licensing and added footprint; request separate approval for new third-party reuse where required.
- Prefer an ordinary console command (illustrative name `SORSnapshot`, actual command subject to evidence) whose handler only initiates a read-only, one-shot capture. It must have clear success/failure output, unique snapshot identifier and output path. Do not rely on implicit background collection. If direct command registration is unavailable under the pinned build, investigate the smallest documented alternative and report the interface trade-off explicitly; do not pretend a different trigger is a console command.
- Before invocation, avoid observer hooks on request publication, scheduler/worker, visited checks, locks and generator. Quantify actual plugin-load and registration effects and avoid startup actions that query or initialize resource populations. No polling or automatic sampling of the generation winner.
- Carefully distinguish command invocation on the game thread from the native area/point-query callback's already-held reader context. **Do not** assume the console thread may directly borrow pointers or acquire locks in arbitrary order. Establish safe acquisition and owner/destructor fencing; copy under valid protection into owned buffers, release all engine state before writing files. No use-after-free, same-thread recursive exclusive lock, stale pointer reuse or wait-for-owner deadlock may be admitted.

### 3.2 Generic identity and coverage

- Identify the player's current outpost through supported runtime identity and verify the native relationship to world, planet/body, current cell/worldspace and its persistent outpost/beacon reference where possible. Names/labels are diagnostics, not identity certificates. Test absent outpost, wrong exterior area, multiple outposts, current outpost without resident generation, and ambiguous or recycled references.
- Reconstruct the **source ownership graph**: actual owning Grid/source vector; source geographic key, current owner ProcGen, current holder/map key; relevant descriptor/frame and the outpost capture area. Avoid assuming a source is owned *by* an outpost merely because it contributes nearby deposits. Store separate identities for outpost, geographic source, source allocation, source-holder map entry and geometry.
- Choose and document a concrete coverage claim, for example 'all presently resident sources with authenticated geometry intersecting the qualified capture area' **only if** the real point/area semantics support that test. A whole-owner map dump may be an additional diagnostic but is not an outpost census. If no reliable complete area-membership test is possible offline, explicitly identify and preserve **resident-map census** and **area association UNKNOWN** rather than silently dropping entries or falsely asserting completeness.
- Capture **multiple source populations** (including unknown or empty holders) in one invocation. Do not require Feynman, one target source, a particular resource FormID, or a preselected number of deposits in the plugin. Provide an explicit diagnostic for target selection only in the offline Feynman analyzer.
- For absent/incomplete/ambiguous source identification, record evidence and a qualified status. Never label silence, empty cache, skipped scan or unavailable data as proven absence of geographical resources without a completed suitable consumer/area observation.

### 3.3 Complete geometry and storage

- Preserve every represented resource FormID, region header, point count and ordered point words, plus relevant source/frame and coordinate-system metadata. Distinguish raw representation from canonicalized comparison; never round, spatially align, reorder region/point sequences or ignore unverified fields merely to produce equality.
- Use a self-describing **versioned** format (binary with sidecar JSON, or an appropriately bounded structured alternative) with schema/method identity, exact game EXE/SFSE/plugin/build identity, process ID **and creation time**, UTC timestamp with subsecond precision, capture UUID, outpost reference and area context, source/holder identities, completeness/quality/refusal codes, resource/region/point counts, contents and hashes. Include wall-clock timestamp for filenames; do not use time alone for uniqueness.
- Each successful invocation writes one **never-overwritten** timestamped snapshot, by writing to a fresh exclusive temporary file, flushing, and publishing a verified complete file atomically where supported. Crashes, partial writes and disk failures must yield an explicitly identifiable partial artifact or failure receipt, never a misleading complete record. No old file deletion or recycling.
- Filenames should be sortable and distinctive (e.g., `YYYYMMDDTHHMMSS.ffffffZ_<session>_<uuid>...`) without exposing private local identifiers unnecessarily. The analysis must not depend on the filename for provenance; verify content and duplicate process/session/snapshot identities. One command must not accidentally produce multiple counted samples.
- Protect against unbounded in-game memory allocation or file IO, but **do not impose arbitrary truncation of scientifically healthy captures**. Define measured required capacity or a preflight bound, and fail explicitly on a genuine capacity limit. Honor 40,000,000,000 **caller-available bytes on each of C: and D:** at relevant acquisition/analysis stages, with honest disk/query failure reporting.
- Snapshot analysis and reference classification occur offline. The plugin should **not** claim direct historical mask identification based on the observed geometry. Unknown/novel geometry is a first-class result, not forced to mask zero or three.

## 4. Offline implementation and proof

Implement minimally invasive native/SFSE sources, a read-only snapshot reader, and a standalone offline decoder/comparator with deterministic, versioned outputs. Follow existing project naming and `AGENTS.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`, `THIRD_PARTY.md`, `SAVE_PROTECTION.md`, `RESEARCH_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md`, `EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md`, `EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`, reviewer and completion-template requirements. **AC01 requires fully recovered substantive reading and independent verification of its application, not reading chronology.** At most two active specialist subagents at once; an independent non-implementing reviewer must examine exact final bytes.

Required executed controls, not just test descriptions:

1. **Native safety and method coverage:** real original-instruction/owned-object cases for reader admission, concurrent mutator, owner destruction, source retirement, map rehash/ABA, nested collection lifetime, recursion, empty/malformed collections, partial copies, complete copy and cleanup. Exercise the actual proposed console-call context or a faithful explicitly bounded surrogate; articulate remaining retail risks.
2. **Genericity:** at least two genuinely distinct synthetic outposts/world contexts; multiple source holders, unrelated map residents, overlapping/intersecting and nonintersecting regions, unknown outpost reference and missing source allocation. No hardcoded Feynman identity in producer code. Prove selected scope and all source dispositions, including negative/ambiguous cases.
3. **Data integrity:** exact raw-to-normalized independent oracle, corrupted/truncated/duplicate frame rejection, wrong frame/owner, altered scalar, resource slot permutation according to **justified** canonicalization, repeated invocation and unique filenames, atomic commit/partial write, failure to write, varied region/point counts and novel valid geometry.
4. **Historical reference comparison:** independently reproduce full normalized mask-3 geometry against D5AK A0 and fixed-input native mask-0/3 controls, with field-level comparisons, **only when** coordinate frame and effective input equivalence actually qualify. Check that equal counts but changed semantic bytes are rejected and that a different source cannot accidentally match the target by hash alone. Do not assume fixed C1 is automatically the retail input state.
5. **Operational absence of interference:** instrumentation-surface inventory; demonstrate no hot scheduling/construction hooks or proactive scan; validate no state write, generation callback, visited update or forced cache refresh. Explicitly characterize residual SFSE/console/pause and snapshot-time effects without claiming zero observer impact.
6. **Exact reproducible build and packaging:** independent source hashes, pinned toolchain/API versions, SFSE metadata, versioned ZIP and DLL, full clean build and byte reproduction where feasible, correct notices and licensing, deliberately conservative product name that cannot be confused with older `SorConstructionWinner` live packages. Package **NOT AUTHORIZED**, cannot silently overwrite installed Vortex content.
7. **Python/operational guards:** use pinned interpreter with `-E -B`, explicit early refusal of `-O`/`-OO`/inherited optimization for safety-sensitive scripts, never depend on assertions for validation or side-effect execution; test refusal and no-output effects. Content-equal timestamp differences are diagnostic and acceptable; actual content changes fail.
8. **Review:** independent substantive native/concurrency challenge, actual control repetition on the final source bytes, correct source/frame/generalization review, complete authored-prose/editorial reading, preservation receipts and exact complete/task-only diff generation with forward/reverse checks.

Do not weaken safety requirements simply for a passing pilot. Equally, **scientific nonqualification of a healthy resident snapshot is not an automatic machine emergency**: preserve narrower evidence, return clear status and hold the dependent statistical conclusion. Actual worker/identity/lifetime failure, corruption, content-protection violation or disk failure retains proportionate protective response.

- **Statistical question:** test the *hypothetical unweighted candidate model* `p(mask-0-equivalent)=28/196=1/7` under *independent and identically distributed* qualified cold starts. Ordinary operator integrity checks (file actually completed; process exited; protected contents preserved) remain mandatory and do not unblind aggregate results. ## 9. Boundary against premature conclusions

D5AR does **not** settle whether Starfield has a mask-3 invariant, the frequency of mask-0 retail outcomes, whether any request is usually preferred, or why resource-generation races resolve as observed. It builds an independently qualified instrument and proposed study capable of testing those questions with lower winner-critical interference. No produced snapshot should be called a direct historical mask observation without recorded direct evidence; no census should be called outpost-specific without native/geographical proof; and no 30-sample statistical conclusion exists before the separately approved, fully qualified series is collected and independently reviewed.
