## 4. Scientific decision and stopping rules

Give a compact, explicit classification:

- **Enumeration defect demonstrated:** Native code/evidence supports a general inclusion rule; independently generated source inventory discovers `(-12, -12)` and explains the original omission.
- **Geometry correspondence demonstrated:** The independently qualified observed-source inputs reproduce all three captured native regions exactly, with controls preserved.
- **Partially discriminated:** One of these conditions is established, or a specific class of omissions is eliminated, while another dependency remains unqualified.
- **Blocked:** Identify the exact missing operand, state or source transition and show why the existing offline evidence cannot establish it. Do not close on a generic statement that runtime context is unknown.

An alternative hypothesis is acceptable if demonstrated with source evidence. Do not require impossible proof of universal enumeration. Do not claim a one-session consumer snapshot is the historical producer state. If further native acquisition is truly necessary, give the **smallest targeted collector extension, synthetic qualification criteria and approval-ready run card**; no live operation is authorized. Do not substitute another broad instrumentation feasibility survey for the actual analysis.
