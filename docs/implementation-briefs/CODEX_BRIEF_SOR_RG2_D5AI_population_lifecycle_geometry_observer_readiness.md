## 1. Mission and non-negotiable scientific distinction

Develop and independently qualify an observer and comparison method that can determine what actually happens to the **complete ordinary resource population of an identified source** across normal departures, returns, and independently initialized game sessions. Prepare—but do not execute—two separately authorized gameplay episodes. Make and freeze prospective, falsifiable predictions **before any Phase B outcome is collected**, then specify a fair, unchanged procedure for comparing predictions to observations.

The instrument must distinguish all four possibilities without substituting one for another:

1. **Retained:** same population survives and is reused, with defensible lifetime/ownership continuity.
2. **Reconstructed identically:** distinct generation/population lifetime, complete normalized resource identity and geometry equal.
3. **Reconstructed differently:** distinct generation/population lifetime, differing identity or complete geometry.
4. **Unavailable:** expected population not accessible to the relevant resource-geometry consumer at the measured boundary, with absence proven at that boundary rather than inferred from a missing observer event.

A successful capture of a mask, unchanged resource list, extractor production, holder pointer equality, or absent event does **not** by itself establish any of these outcomes. Native possibility, demonstrated fixture operation, and actual retail occurrence must have different evidence labels.

**Excluded separate investigative lines:** extractor placement, ongoing production and resource association (retain D5AH's findings as a separate extractor backlog); intersection/non-overlap of physically distinct resource deposits. Do not make either a gate for completing this assignment. A minimal read-only resource-consumer witness needed to establish geometry availability may be investigated, without broadening into extractor behaviour.

## 2. Controlling questions — freeze wording and test conditions

**D5AI-Q1 — Lifecycle and attribution.** Can the instrument safely and positively identify a particular geographical resource source, its population holder and geometry across construction, publication, retention, retirement, reset and reconstruction, with source/holder/allocation/owner/visited identities kept distinct? Success means independent oracles and adverse native tests establish that old/new population lifetimes cannot be silently conflated. A missing indispensable native ownership edge is not a pass.

**D5AI-Q2 — Complete geometry and availability.** Can the instrument capture and normalize *all* applicable ordinary resource entries, region records and their constituent points, and compare complete populations without losing relevant distinctions or confusing differing coordinate frames? Can it distinguish a population that exists but is not currently exposed to the relevant ordinary geometry query from one genuinely unavailable there? Success requires complete independent geometry oracles, error controls, and explicit normalizer rules.

**D5AI-Q3 — Retirement/visited anomaly.** What do the native lifecycle and consumer paths actually guarantee if source retirement removes a population-map entry but leaves the source's visited identity? Which additional conditions would permit a recreated equivalent source to bypass generation, what paths could prevent or repair this, and what **specific observable results** would follow? Prove the stated predicates and tested consequences within their scope. Do not claim this chain occurs ordinarily unless observed in retail; do not claim crash, missing resource or geometry change without relevant consumer evidence. Maintain an explicit **unverified-anomaly record**.

**D5AI-Q4 — Prospective experiment readiness and predictions.** Can the final package and the proposed Episode A/B procedures discriminate retention, identical/different reconstruction, and unavailability without forcing regeneration? Does a separately reviewed, immutable prediction register make at least one substantive, falsifiable forecast about future observations, supported by pre-existing evidence and evaluable by the proposed package? Success is *readiness for a later decision*, not a gameplay outcome or evidence that the prediction is correct.

The broader gameplay goals **GC-25** (actual retained geometry), **GC-30** (departure/return mechanism), and **GC-31** (independent reproducibility) remain OPEN until their original gameplay-level standards are met. GC-10 and D5R remain OPEN independently. Do not artificially require complete retail results within an offline assignment.

## 4. Stage A — offline native investigation and engineering

### A1. Map the ownership and measured lifecycle

Draw an evidence-indexed transition matrix for source identities, visited tokens, pointer-map entries, population holders, nested resource collections and nested geometry allocations. Separate source retirement from holder release and from broad ProcGen reset; account for reader leases, asynchronous consumers and concurrent source reconstruction. Reuse established D5AB ownership proofs but **requalify all new nested geometry accesses**. An earlier lifetime proof for a source pointer is not a lease on every nested geometry allocation.

Identify qualified capture points at a coherent population completion or consumption boundary. Prefer small, bounded, ownership-safe snapshots over repeated geometry copying in high-frequency hooks. Explicitly analyze geometry modification/partial copy, allocation failure, end-of-generation state, parallel readers/writers, destruction, ABA/pointer reuse, interruptions, and reset fencing. Instrument only where the source/holder/collection lifetime can be proven or use an alternative with equivalent evidentiary strength. No unsafe post-hoc pointer traversal.

### A2. Geometry serialization, comparison and provenance

Define a versioned observation schema and independent consumer/checker that preserves **resource FormID, all relevant region fields, region order or justified canonical order, all points and their coordinates, owner/source frame and any geometry-affecting flags**. Specify exact floating-point handling, padding treatment, byte/semantic equality and whether order is meaningful; do not use a weak geometry hash as sole equality proof. Hashes may assist integrity, but any claimed equality must be reproducible by complete normalized content comparison. No unauthorized omission of zero-length, partial, invalid or allocation-failed populations. Source map holder identity and population content equality are orthogonal.

Use fixed-input native geometry examples including the known Feynman V **mask 0 vs mask 3** difference and equal mask-3 outcomes. Verify original-vs-normalized comparison against independent ground truth; reject truncated region sets, wrong frame translations, missing points, FormID substitutions, incorrect ordering assumptions, and malformed/crashed snapshots. Scientific renderer/map figures are optional, not substitutes for machine-checkable geometry.

### A3. Unverified retirement/visited anomaly

Produce `d5ai-anomaly-register.md`, explicitly separating (a) proven compiled predicates, (b) conditions that may prevent the chain in normal gameplay, (c) fixture-demonstrated paths, (d) actually observed retail transitions, and (e) unobserved consequences. Address retirement → source recreation → surviving visited identity → skipped construction → current population availability; investigate retained alternate owners, epoch/reset ordering, cache re-establishment and appropriate geometry consumer behaviour. Give each outcome a discriminating observation. A crash is not the default prediction and must not be inferred from a missing map entry. Any necessary adverse native control uses owned disposable host objects only.

### A4. Transport and operational qualification

Build on the existing accepted schema-4 source-identity, queue, worker/controller, protection and reduction infrastructure. Recheck actual event size, queue capacity, producer hot-path overhead, nested-data capture cost, worker latency and sampled/uncounted loss modes. Validate concurrent producers, held worker, source retirement during snapshot, partial allocation, malformed data, bounded stress, queue overflow, I/O faults, worker termination, unexpected process exit, STOP and clean finalization. Preserve loss/uncertainty semantics; a zero-drop fixture is not a retail throughput guarantee. Do not introduce arbitrary total-event, evidence-size or operator-delay limits. Preserve **40,000,000,000 caller-available bytes on both C: and D:** and all existing save, identity, guard, session, archive and STOP protection requirements.

Any changed observer schema, bridge, controller or consumer requires exact producer/reader/reducer correspondence, versioned backward compatibility (including B1 schema-2 and B2 schema-4 where relevant), independent adversarial fixtures, actual compiled-native tests, and a complete source/build/artifact identity chain. Do not silently rewrite historical captures or scientific classifications.
