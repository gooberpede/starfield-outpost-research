## Required design and work

### A. Minimum viable instrumentation, safety first

1. Establish exact candidate hook sites from the pinned executable and native instruction/ownership paths. Maintain a hook inventory: RVA/VA, original bytes, displaced instructions, live register/stack contracts, site-specific safety, alternative rejected sites, synchronized source lifetimes, and costs. Do **not** revive unsafe Grid `count <= 256` scans, traverse moving backing storage without proven ownership, or read selected source pointers after source retirement. D5S's detached-density path shows Grid retention alone is insufficient.
2. Prefer data *already consumed by the game under qualified protection*: source identity token (`+0x68` where qualified), source key from protected source copy, source constructor identity operands, append reserve/store operand, or a demonstrably stable allocation cookie joined from construction/publication to retirement. Clearly separate 8-byte visited token, geographic source key, raw pointer, allocation identity, and lifetime epoch; detect collisions and re-use without equating identities. If a critical identity edge cannot be proven safe, report the exact native boundary and evaluate safe alternate placements before escalation.
3. Instrument actual **reserve result and token write**. Capture successful store only when the store genuinely executes; record failed reserve/skip and count/capacity, relevant exception/status, epoch and thread/invocation. Preserve the distinction between append attempt and successful insertion, and avoid unsafe logging within locks or allocation paths. Hooks must forward originals with preserved semantics; guard against recursion and throwing inside hot-path callbacks.
4. Retain global ProcGen reset and protected empty-state unlock, identity constructors, source retirement/erasure/Grid invalidation, and all relevant provider/descriptor/mask boundaries. Preserve enough earlier non-target events to establish cold firstness. **Do not filter by guessed target address, pointer or geography**. If sampling/rate reduction is proposed, demonstrate it cannot remove required lineage or claim firstness when suppression exists; a better unfiltered design is preferred.
5. Consider narrow native transition markers (pending travel publication/consumption, owner/descriptor setup, focus/registration/claim, and optionally a direct engine loading/arrival boundary) only when necessary and safely qualifiable. Mark events as engine-native versus operator-reported, with QPC ticks and thread/parent context. Do not expand hooks without demonstrated information gain and safety proof.
