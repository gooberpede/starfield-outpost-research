## Purpose and frozen scientific contract

**Parent questions:** D5R remains OPEN. **GC-10** remains unchecked: *Determine which eligible construction request normally constructs first under retail readiness and scheduling.* GC-31 (repeatability across independent cold episodes) is separate, and GC-06 (travel-to-demand causality) is an upstream dependency where relevant. D5AE, though accepted as a bounded offline investigation, did **not** establish why ordinary outcomes appear geometrically stable or why B2's two requests arrived at the protected map in their observed order. Do not equate acceptance of D5AF's scoped findings with completion of GC-10 or D5R.

**D5AF-CQ — Primary hypothesis:** *Ordinary resource generation contains one or more native ordering, eligibility, synchronization, or other positively evidenced mechanisms that systematically favor particular constructing requests and thereby promote reproducible **complete resource-deposit geometry**, not merely the same available resource identities.* Identify the mechanism, its exact domain, how it affects which request obtains construction authority, and falsifiable predictions. The work must specifically explain—or isolate the missing discriminating variable behind—the observed B2 ordering reversal. This is a testable hypothesis, **not a conclusion granted in advance**.

**Consistency standard:** Generated populations are consistent only when their **resource identities and complete deposit geometry** agree under an explicit coordinate frame and equivalent effective inputs. Identical resource sets with shifted regions are **not** consistent: the changed geometry could invalidate extractor locations. Do not claim two requests are equivalent merely because their masks match without confirming other effective generation inputs, or claim two different masks equivalent without comparing complete generated geometry. Existing retention without regeneration is a separate persistence mechanism, **not** evidence that independent constructions are reproducible. We should establish the basic winner-selection mechanism first; investigate equivalence/edge cases only if positive evidence makes them necessary.

**Frozen controlling questions (do not narrow without maintainer approval):**

- **D5AF-Q1 — Pre-lock competition:** What native instructions, synchronization ownership, waits, scheduling handoffs, and material computation lie between a selected source's visited check and its protected pointer-map decision? For B2's target `0:37536:20928:0029d20e`, why did `[4,-1]` (invocation 3837, visited decision 8006) precede `[5,0]` (invocation 3844, visited decision 8020), yet `[5,0]` obtain constructing miss 8091 before `[4,-1]` obtained hit 8339? **COMPLETE** requires a causal, evidence-tested explanation or identification of a precise missing discriminating execution edge—not generic invocation of concurrency, the OS, or a hypothetical lock wait.
- **D5AF-Q2 — Stabilizing candidate(s):** Is there an identifiable native mechanism that systematically constrains or favors the winner under ordinary gameplay? Investigate, guided by actual code/evidence, request preparation and cost, geographic ingress/registration, readiness dependencies, worker affinity/locality, queue and claim discipline, priority, preemption, lock admission and relevant reset/state fences. Prioritize mechanisms capable of distinguishing **same-source competing requests**; do not re-map the entire task scheduler without a demonstrated dependency. For each credible candidate establish the exact relevant instructions/state and what advantage it confers, or falsify it with an independent counterexample. **COMPLETE** requires tracing the material supported candidates to a substantiated result or an evidenced terminal limitation, not merely listing them.
- **D5AF-Q3 — Geometric reproducibility and discriminating predictions:** Determine whether the candidate mechanism, under explicit equivalent cold initial conditions, promotes reproducible *complete geometry*, distinguishing (a) deterministic winner, (b) systematic but not absolute preference, (c) differently winning requests that are proved equivalent in full effective generation result, and (d) within-session retention without regeneration. Make falsifiable predictions about request/lock/winner/mask and, where geometry claims are made, complete population output. Test B2 and independent contrasting native controls. **COMPLETE** requires a supported predictive causal contract or an evidence-supported alternative explanation with equivalent scientific discrimination; vague assertions about apparently stable gameplay or same resource lists cannot close it.
- **D5AF-Q4 — Evidence sufficiency and future observation gate:** Identify exactly which parts of the mechanism are established by static native evidence, executable offline controls, B2's one retail episode, and player observation; what is still unobserved; and which runtime operands/events would test a specific falsifiable candidate **if** a later live experiment were authorized. **COMPLETE** requires an actionable candidate-to-prediction-to-measurement matrix and explicit tests that would disprove the leading explanation. **No live trial may be recommended merely to collect more winners without a credible candidate and measurable discriminator.**

**Hypothesis discipline:** Pursue the stabilization hypothesis seriously. An alternative explanation may be promoted to a competing model only with **positive supporting native evidence or established empirical evidence**; speculative alternative mechanisms are not grounds for deferring or closing the main investigation. Equally, do not manufacture proof of stabilization because gameplay appears stable. If a candidate fails, pursue the next evidenced material branch in this assignment. A controlled negative for a narrow candidate is not completion of D5AF-CQ. Distinguish “no stabilizer found yet” from “adequately demonstrated no material stabilizer in the scoped graph,” and preserve unresolved high-value paths.
