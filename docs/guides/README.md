# Subsystem guides

Start with the question you want to answer. The guides explain ordinary
behavior, its saved-state boundary and known exceptions.

- [Outpost identity and membership](identity.md) follows owner links and construction roots.
- [Planetary location and position](position.md) explains saved sites, frames and terrain cells.
- [Nominal biomes](nominal-biomes.md) explains discrete coverage and its limits.
- [Construction and placement](construction.md) follows catalogue choices through placement and persistence.
- [Resource generation](resource-generation.md) explains why deposits can
  repeat or change and how the result affects building and extractors.
- [Inorganic extraction](inorganic-production.md) follows placement, the
  registered resource and the path to a receiving inventory.
- [Organic production](organic-production.md) explains planetary species,
  menu knowledge and greenhouse and husbandry inputs.
- [Fabrication](fabrication.md) follows product presets, recipe changes
  and the local supply of ingredients.
- [Power and generator fuel](power.md) explains ratings, fuel, wiring,
  switches and which devices can be powered during a shortage.
- [Storage and local output links](storage-and-output-links.md) follows physical stock, capacity and directed movement.
- [Cargo links and fuel](cargo.md) follows the shared hold, return freight and inter-system readiness.
- [Shared Outpost Storage](shared-storage.md) explains fixture access, ingredient sources and spending.
- [Locations Stored Resources](stored-resources.md) explains eligible inventories, unit totals and refresh.
- [Crew and robots](crew-and-robots.md) explains assignment, capacity and when skills contribute.
- [Outpost defense](defense.md) follows attack checks, combat, damage and repair.
- [Containers and displays](containers-and-displays.md) explains stacks, displayed references and mannequin equipment.
- [The Locations outpost panel](locations-ui.md) explains badges, system imagery and refresh.
- [Access and save framing](access-and-save-framing.md) explains where facts come from and what the established interfaces support.
