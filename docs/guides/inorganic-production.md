# Inorganic extraction

An ordinary extractor built over a deposit acquires its resource from the
machine's effective resource properties. The ground matters when you place the
machine. After registration, the Workshop schedules the placed producer under
its resource key. The mapped update uses that key when it requests items. This
explains how a previously built extractor can remain configured even when a
new extractor at the same point would fail the resource check. The project has
yet to observe complete geometry change and continued output together for one
machine. The native account applies to the mapped Starfield 1.16.244.0 paths.

## From ground to a production request

An iron extractor's base defines a resource property. That property identifies
the iron resource form, which points to a production recipe and an inventory
item. At placement, the ordinary natural-extractor check asks whether at least
one resource the machine can produce is admitted at the chosen point. Other
building rules still apply. An area resource list can show iron even if the
chosen point does not admit it. Atmospheric variants, liquid water and certain
expansion definitions have other selectors or bypass properties. See the
[resource generation guide](resource-generation.md) for contours and the
[extractor definitions](../experiments/inorganic-extractors/definitions.md) for
exceptions.

Once the Workshop has registered a placed machine, a scheduler advances its
resource-keyed row using engine elapsed time. At the applicable production
check the engine resolves the machine and evaluates current eligibility.
Power matters on the branch that requires it. A production halt and a full
destination are distinct reasons a machine may fail to operate. A qualified
request can create the product directly at a selected connected store or in
the extractor's own buffer. If you inspect only the extractor, an empty buffer
can therefore coexist with successful production. Local transfer of existing
stock is a separate [local output-link operation](storage-and-output-links.md).

## Compare two nominal intervals

In the tracked Starfield 1.16.244.0 tables, Iron and He-3 both link to a
36-engine-second base interval. With the default timer property, no instance
override or additional contribution, and no active actor/player timer entry,
the small solid iron base uses a duration factor of 1 and small gas He-3
uses 2. The respective nominal durations are 36 and 72 engine elapsed
seconds per cycle. With one registration iteration and sufficient eligibility
and capacity, the conditional nominal requests are 60/36 and 60/72 units per
engine elapsed minute. These are arithmetic comparisons under fixed
assumptions. Actual inventory growth depends on update timing and insertion.

## What survives a save

A qualified save can preserve the placed reference and its base, the Workshop
resource key, producer identity and timer bookkeeping, and separately the
local links. The loader resolves the identities into current references and
handles. Current gates are evaluated again. A saved cached eligibility byte
answers a historical question. A saved resource key identifies the producer's
association rather than the vein shape or the machine's current production.
The iron placement/deletion controls and the mature Save52 extractor examples
give concrete saved associations. Their raw inputs are outside the tracked
reader check. [Saved controls](../experiments/resource-retained-population/d5ah-extractor-continuity.md#saved-controls-and-a-concrete-future-candidate).
